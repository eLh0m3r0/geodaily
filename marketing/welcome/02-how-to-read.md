Subject: How to read this brief in 5 minutes (and why the sources matter)

Quick guide to getting the most out of Geopolitical Daily, now that you've
had a few editions:

**The Big Story** is the one thing worth your full attention today. Two or
three short paragraphs, no jargon. If you read nothing else, read this.

**The source links** under each story are not decoration. They tell you who
is reporting a story — and, just as importantly, who isn't. When a story
cites only outlets from one country or one political lens, that itself is
information. We deliberately mix wire services, regional outlets and
specialist analysis, and we never cite the same outlet twice under one story.

**"What Others Are Missing"** is where we flag what the dominant framing
leaves out — the underreported actor, the longer arc, the blind spot.

That's it. Five minutes, and you're ahead of most of the people you'll
talk to today.
