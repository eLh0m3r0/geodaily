Kenya confirms its first Ebola death as Congo's outbreak spreads

Africa Humanitarian
12 reports from 10 outlets · Western media 2 · East Asian media 1 · Chinese state media 1 · South Asian media 3 · Russian state media 1 · African media 4

Why This Matters

A Kenyan man who travelled from eastern Congo via Uganda died of Ebola in Nairobi, Kenya's first confirmed case. Congo's outbreak is the second largest on record, with more than 8,000 cases, and Uganda has already had 20. Kenya's health system now faces its own first outbreak.

What Others Are Missing

The man crossed at least one international border before he died. Kenya is the fourth country linked to Congo's outbreak, which shows how easily the virus gets past border screening.

What to Watch

Kenya's health ministry will report whether the dead man's contacts test positive. A cluster would mean local transmission, not an imported case.
