Pakistan and Turkey agree to send troops to Saudi Arabia

Middle East Military

Why This Matters

Pakistan, Turkey and Saudi Arabia agreed to move troops and military equipment into Saudi Arabia. They agreed to do so quickly under the Makkah defense pact. The decision follows weeks of Houthi missile and drone attacks on Saudi Arabia. It also condemns attempted strikes on Mecca and Medina.

What to Watch

If troops arrive in Saudi Arabia within a week, the alliance is real. If only statements follow, it is mostly symbolic.

Africa Military
