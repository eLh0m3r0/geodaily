The US will bring Russian and Ukrainian delegations together in Abu Dhabi on Nov. 10-11. They will discuss ending the war, post-war security and how to lower tensions. US envoy Steve Witkoff announced the working-level talks, the first such three-way meeting since February.

Cyberattack disables supermarket fridges across Europe

EuropeInformational Cyber
14 reports from 12 outlets · Western media 6 · East Asian media 3 · South Asian media 2 · Russian state media 2 · African media 1

Why This Matters

A cyberattack on Danfoss refrigeration systems hit supermarkets in at least nine European countries. It raised temperatures and forced food to be thrown away, and the EU warned the incident appears deliberate. The same maker's systems also came under attack in France last month.

What Others Are Missing

The attacks are hitting the same kind of equipment from one company. That suggests attackers are reusing a known method, not carrying out isolated hacks.

What to Watch

A second wave of hacks could show whether a government is behind this. Danfoss or an EU agency naming the attackers would also show it.
