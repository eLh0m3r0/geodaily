Why This Matters

The COP31 talks in Antalya ended Sunday with no final agreement at all. The EU and other countries rejected a text from hosts Turkey and Australia. They said it was too weak on fossil fuels and on money for adaptation. The 30-year UN process now faces open doubt.

What Others Are Missing

Negotiators from the countries most at risk openly asked whether these yearly summits can still deliver, Reuters reported. Doubt now comes from inside the process, not just from its critics.

What to Watch

Whether Turkey and Australia call a resumed session before year end will show if the UN process can still deliver any deal.
