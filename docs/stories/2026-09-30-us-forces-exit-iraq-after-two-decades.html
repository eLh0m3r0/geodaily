US forces exit Iraq after two decades

September 30, 2026

Why This Matters

The last US forces left their remaining bases in Iraq on Wednesday. This ends a deployment that began in 2014 to fight the Islamic State. The pullout is mostly popular among Iraqis, but it raises fear of a security gap. Iran's allies and ISIS can exploit that gap.

What Others Are Missing

President Biden's administration agreed to the timetable in 2024, and President Trump's administration carried it out. Supporters on both sides skip the fact it continued, and Tehran called the exit a victory.

What to Watch

Whether Iraqi forces take over the vacated bases or Iran-backed militias move in first will show who controls the west.
