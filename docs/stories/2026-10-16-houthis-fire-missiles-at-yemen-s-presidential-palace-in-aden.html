Houthis fire missiles at Yemen's presidential palace in Aden

October 16, 2026

Why This Matters

Houthi missiles hit the Maashiq presidential palace complex in Aden, home of Yemen's internationally recognized government. The strike killed guards and staff, but the prime minister and cabinet escaped harm. This strike widens a war that has already hit Riyadh's airport and an Aramco site. It worries oil markets and Saudi plans to attract foreign investment.

What Others Are Missing

Casualty counts differ: Yemen's health minister said five died and 16 were hurt, while Reuters reported two security guards killed. The Houthis called the building a 'Saudi war operations room'.

What to Watch

Saudi Arabia will strike Houthi-held areas within days; a hit on Sanaa or Hodeidah ports would signal the war is widening further.
