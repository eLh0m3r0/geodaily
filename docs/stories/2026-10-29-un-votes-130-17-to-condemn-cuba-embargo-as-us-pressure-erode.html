UN votes 130-17 to condemn Cuba embargo as US pressure erodes support

October 29, 2026

Why This Matters

The UN General Assembly voted 130-17, with 25 abstentions, to condemn the US embargo on Cuba for the 34th year. Support fell sharply from more than 160 votes last year, after Ambassador Mike Waltz led a US lobbying campaign.

What Others Are Missing

The resolution does not bind anyone, so no US law changes; it serves as a scoreboard of which governments still side with Havana against Washington.

What to Watch

Whether Washington penalizes any of the 130 countries that voted yes will show if its lobbying campaign has teeth.
