Germany's Far Right Could Win Its First State Government

September 05, 2026

Why This Matters

The AfD leads polls ahead of Sunday's Saxony-Anhalt election. A win would give the far-right party control of a German state for the first time since World War Two. It would test how far the political center can hold in Europe's biggest economy.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on the AfD's rise in the east but underplays how a state win boosts the party's push nationally next year.

What to Watch

Expect exit polls Sunday night showing AfD near or above 35-40%, triggering new coalition scrambles.
