Pakistan imposes emergency rule and suspends Imran Khan's provincial government

October 12, 2026

Why This Matters

Pakistan's president declared a state of emergency and suspended the government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Jailed former prime minister Imran Khan's party runs the province. The federal government said the province failed to fight Islamist militants along the Afghan border.

What Others Are Missing

Khan's PTI party called the move undemocratic, and the province is the center of his support and borders Taliban-run Afghanistan.

What to Watch

Whether Imran Khan's party can overturn the suspension in court this week will show how far Pakistan's government will go.
