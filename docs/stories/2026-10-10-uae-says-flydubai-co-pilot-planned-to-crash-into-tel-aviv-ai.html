UAE says flydubai co-pilot planned to crash into Tel Aviv airport

October 10, 2026

Why This Matters

The UAE's top prosecutor said an Omani co-pilot on a flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv planned to crash into Ben Gurion airport. He said the co-pilot was inspired by the September 11 attacks. Passengers described a sudden plunge and a fight for control before an emergency landing in Saudi Arabia.

What Others Are Missing

The prosecutor said the co-pilot flew the Dubai-Tel Aviv route twice before to study the airport. A second flydubai flight took passengers home hours later.

What to Watch

Whether the UAE names the co-pilot and any al-Qaeda contacts will show if this was a lone plot or part of a wider network.
