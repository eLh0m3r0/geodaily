Venezuela's Machado returns home to a government that has not called elections

October 26, 2026

Why This Matters

María Corina Machado is Venezuela's opposition leader and a Nobel Prize winner. She returned home Sunday after ten months in exile and seven failed attempts. Tens of thousands greeted her convoy in Caracas, while acting president Delcy Rodríguez has stayed in office past her 90-day limit without calling elections.

What Others Are Missing

Her failed return attempts had already hurt relations with the Trump administration, which backs Caracas's temporary government. Her arrival now tests whether Washington wants an election.

What to Watch

Whether Venezuelan authorities allow Machado to hold public rallies this week, or move to detain or expel her.
