United Russia wins a record Duma majority as the EU prepares sanctions

September 22, 2026

Why This Matters

United Russia won 57.83% of the vote, up from 49.8% in 2021. The EU says the election was not free and will sanction organizers and candidates who ran in occupied Ukrainian territory.

What Others Are Missing

The anti-war Yabloko party won a small foothold in one regional parliament, a detail most coverage skipped.

What to Watch

Watch when the EU names the sanctioned officials, and whether Moscow answers with its own measures against European monitors.
