Oil Jumps to $108 as Middle East Attacks Multiply

September 14, 2026

Why This Matters

Brent crude jumped more than $3 in a day after new strikes hit Saudi Arabia and a ship in the Strait of Hormuz. That is the fastest oil spike in months. Higher oil means higher prices everywhere, from gas pumps to shipping costs, and shows how fast Gulf tensions can hit ordinary wallets worldwide.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on prices but underplays that Gulf states have now postponed talks with Iran entirely, removing a key off-ramp.

What to Watch

Watch whether Brent crude breaks $110 if attacks on Gulf shipping or Saudi facilities continue this week.
