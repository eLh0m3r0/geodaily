Israeli settlers attack European diplomats in the West Bank

October 18, 2026

Why This Matters

Israeli settlers attacked a convoy of European diplomats and beat a visiting French mayor during the Palestinian olive harvest. Belgium says it will push for EU sanctions on those responsible. Secretary of State Marco Rubio warned of a West Bank crisis ahead of Israel's October 27 election.

What Others Are Missing

US officials say settlers have made at least 170 attacks on Palestinians since September 27, a pace previous olive harvests rarely saw.

What to Watch

Whether EU foreign ministers start formal sanctions talks this week will show if Belgium's push has real backing.
