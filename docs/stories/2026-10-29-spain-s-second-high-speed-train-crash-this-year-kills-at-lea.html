Spain's second high-speed train crash this year kills at least 68

October 29, 2026

Why This Matters

Two high-speed trains collided near Urda station in Toledo province on Wednesday morning, killing at least 68 people. It was Europe's deadliest rail crash since 2013 and Spain's second this year. The transport minister pointed to a computer failure followed by human error, while critics attack the government over rail safety.

What Others Are Missing

Protesters outside the transport ministry, including far-right groups, demanded that Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez and minister Óscar Puente resign, and police used tear gas.

What to Watch

Investigators will identify the failed computer or signal system within 72 hours; whether the infrastructure was state-run will decide the political damage.
