Houthi strike kills 53 at Yemen army camp as group claims attacks on Saudi Arabia

October 20, 2026

Why This Matters

A ballistic missile hit a training camp in Shabwa, killing at least 53 new recruits and wounding 200. It was one of the deadliest attacks since fighting resumed this summer. The Houthis also claim six strikes on Saudi Arabia in 48 hours, and US embassies are warning staff.

What Others Are Missing

Egypt delayed a vote on military support for Saudi Arabia. Riyadh moved its 'Davos in the Desert' summit to next year, so Gulf governments are bracing for more.

What to Watch

Will the Saudi-led coalition strike back inside Yemen within 72 hours? A wide air campaign would take this beyond missile exchanges.
