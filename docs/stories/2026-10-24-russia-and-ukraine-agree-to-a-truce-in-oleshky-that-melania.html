Russia and Ukraine agree to a truce in Oleshky that Melania Trump says she brokered

October 24, 2026

Why This Matters

Moscow and Kyiv said they will stop firing on the Kherson front from Tuesday so civilians trapped in Russian-occupied Oleshky can leave. Melania Trump said she brokered the truce, which both militaries confirmed.

What Others Are Missing

Non-military teams from both countries will visit homes and arrange transport, and an unnamed American news organization will accompany them to document the evacuations.

What to Watch

Whether both sides stop firing at 4 a.m. Tuesday will show if this truce can be repeated elsewhere on the front.
