Heavy rain floods 70,000 tents for displaced Palestinians in Gaza

5 reports from 4 outlets · East Asian media 1 · South Asian media 2 · Middle East media 2

Why This Matters

Heavy rain flooded about 70,000 tents sheltering displaced people in Gaza on the first day of winter rains. The floods left tens of thousands of people without shelter. The Hamas-run government media office also reported collapsed walls and roofs. Some injuries were critical, and war continues to devastate the territory.
