Spain calls snap election after housing protests

October 05, 2026

Why This Matters

Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez called early elections for November 29 after Parliament rejected his housing decrees. The vote could end one of Europe's last left-wing governments as the far-right gains across the continent.

What Others Are Missing

The housing crisis has fueled protests across Spain. The opposition People's Party has not yet offered a clear alternative, leaving room for the far-right Vox to gain.

What to Watch

Sánchez will try to pass a new housing package before the election; if he fails, Vox may gain more in the polls.
