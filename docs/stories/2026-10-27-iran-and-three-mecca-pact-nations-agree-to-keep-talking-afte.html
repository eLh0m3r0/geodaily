Iran and three Mecca Pact nations agree to keep talking after first Tehran meeting

October 27, 2026

Why This Matters

Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan sent senior officials to Tehran. They held their first high-level contact with Iran since a war began with US and Israeli strikes on Iran. That war spread to Saudi Arabia and Yemen, and all four agreed to keep meeting.

What Others Are Missing

The three are also building military strength under the Mecca defense pact, Euronews reported. So the talks run alongside plans for more fighting, not instead of them.

What to Watch

A second meeting with a date attached would show these talks are real. No follow-up within days suggests the first talks were mostly symbolic.
