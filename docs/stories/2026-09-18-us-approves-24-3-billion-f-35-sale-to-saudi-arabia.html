US approves $24.3 billion F-35 sale to Saudi Arabia

September 18, 2026

Why This Matters

The State Department approved selling up to 48 F-35 stealth jets to Saudi Arabia, a deal worth $24.3 billion. Congress can still block it. It would be the first time an Arab state gets America's top fighter, and Israel opposes it.

What Others Are Missing

Congress can still block the sale, and Israel's objections have not been settled. Riyadh also has no formal US defense treaty.

What to Watch

Watch whether pro-Israel lawmakers move to block the sale in Congress within days.
