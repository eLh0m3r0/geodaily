US wins permanent control of Greenland's security in deal with Denmark

September 19, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump dropped his threat to take over Greenland. Instead, the US and Denmark struck a deal giving Washington permanent control of the territory's security. The deal aims to keep Russian and Chinese bases out of the Arctic and ends a crisis with NATO allies.

What Others Are Missing

Almost all details come from Trump's own social media post. Denmark has not released the text, and Greenland's own government has not clearly agreed.

What to Watch

Watch whether Denmark publishes the deal text and whether Greenland's parliament backs it.
