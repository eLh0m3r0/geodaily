Delegates walk out as Netanyahu dismisses Gaza genocide charge at UN

September 25, 2026

Why This Matters

Dozens of delegates walked out of the UN General Assembly on Thursday. This happened as Netanyahu began speaking. He called accusations of genocide in Gaza 'the biggest lie of the century.' He called those who left 'moral cowards.' The walkout shows Israel is isolated. An election in weeks could end Netanyahu's career.

What Others Are Missing

Most coverage focuses on the walkout drama. Fewer note that Netanyahu downplayed settler violence in the West Bank. He defended strikes on Iran as needed for national survival.

What to Watch

Watch if more European states recognize Palestinian statehood. Also watch if Netanyahu's Gaza policy changes before Israel's October election.
