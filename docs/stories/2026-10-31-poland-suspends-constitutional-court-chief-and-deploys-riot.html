Poland suspends constitutional court chief and deploys riot police

October 31, 2026

Why This Matters

Poland's government suspended the head of the Constitutional Tribunal, and police sealed the building, prompting protests and coup accusations. The clash between Prime Minister Donald Tusk and conservative opponents over judicial control could reshape how the EU treats Warsaw. It could also test the rule of law in a key NATO state.

What Others Are Missing

Police detained protesters, The Hindu reports, a detail missing from most early accounts.

What to Watch

Poland's parliament or president will respond to the suspension; a formal court challenge would show whether the government can make the move stick.
