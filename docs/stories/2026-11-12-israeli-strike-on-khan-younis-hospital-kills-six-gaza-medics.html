Israeli strike on Khan Younis hospital kills six, Gaza medics say

Why This Matters

An airstrike hit a building at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis before dawn Thursday. It killed at least six people, including a surgeon, and wounded about 20. Israel said it targeted a senior Hamas commander working inside the hospital.

What Others Are Missing

Israel says it widened the campaign because Hamas is regrouping and rearming. The toll comes from Gaza's civil defence, not an outside count.
