A Tanker Just Got Hit in Hormuz — and Gulf Shipping Is Grinding to a Halt

August 25, 2026

Why This Matters

A projectile struck an oil tanker off Oman early Tuesday. Only one commodity ship crossed the Strait of Hormuz on Monday, the lowest count since May. Iran's blockade threat is now backed by real attacks, not just words.

What Others Are Missing

Most coverage still frames this as sanctions drama. The real story is that physical oil shipping has nearly stopped, which hits markets faster than any sanctions rule.

What to Watch

Watch oil prices and tanker insurance rates this week, plus whether more ships get hit or reroute entirely.
