Europe names negotiators for Ukraine peace talks

Why This Matters

The EU, Germany, France, the UK, Italy and Poland will send national security advisers to represent Europe in any Russia-Ukraine talks. The move aims to give Europe a unified voice alongside US-led negotiations.

What Others Are Missing

Ukraine has said it wants European backup, but the group has no power to negotiate without US and Russian agreement.
