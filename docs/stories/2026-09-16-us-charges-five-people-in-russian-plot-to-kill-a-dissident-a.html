US Charges Five People in Russian Plot to Kill a Dissident and Bomb Targets in Europe

September 16, 2026

Why This Matters

US prosecutors say Russian spies recruited people to kill a Russian dissident in Washington. They also planned bombings in Europe. This is the strongest US proof yet that Moscow runs killing and sabotage missions in the West. It happened the same week as rail sabotage in the Netherlands and a flare incident near a Danish ship.
