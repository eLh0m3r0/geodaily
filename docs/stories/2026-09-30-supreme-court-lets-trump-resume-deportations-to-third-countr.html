Supreme Court lets Trump resume deportations to third countries

September 30, 2026

Why This Matters

A 6-3 Supreme Court order lets the administration keep sending migrants to countries where they have no ties. It does not first let them object, and the court hears arguments in December. So the policy can run for months before the justices rule.

What Others Are Missing

A federal appeals court is separately weighing whether the administration defied a judge. That judge ordered planes carrying Venezuelans to El Salvador to turn around in March 2025.

What to Watch

Whether immigration authorities send new notices this week to remove migrants to a third country will show how fast they use the order.
