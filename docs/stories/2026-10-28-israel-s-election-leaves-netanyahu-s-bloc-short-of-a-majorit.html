Israel's election leaves Netanyahu's bloc short of a majority

October 28, 2026

Why This Matters

With more than half the votes counted, Netanyahu's right-wing bloc leads with about 56 seats and is short of the 61 needed to govern. Gadi Eisenkot's opposition bloc trails at about 48, while Arab parties hold 16 seats that could decide who becomes prime minister.

What Others Are Missing

Arab communities reported ballot shortages and police disruptions on election day, Haaretz reported. That is a hurdle for parties whose seats may now decide the government.

What to Watch

Official results, expected within days, will show which bloc can assemble 61 seats.
