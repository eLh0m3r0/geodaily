EU and UK sign post-Brexit deal on food, carbon and youth mobility

13 reports from 12 outlets · Western media 8 · East Asian media 2 · South Asian media 1 · Regional state media 1 · Latin American media 1

Why This Matters

Ten years after Brexit, the UK and EU signed their biggest package since Britain left. It covers food trade, permits for carbon emissions and movement of young people. Prime Minister Andy Burnham said this is as close as the UK can get to the EU until 2029.

What Others Are Missing

The deal does not bring back free movement or single-market membership. It eases specific barriers but keeps most Brexit trade rules.

What to Watch

The UK Parliament will publish the deal's legal text within 72 hours. Opposition amendments would show whether Burnham can sell it at home.
