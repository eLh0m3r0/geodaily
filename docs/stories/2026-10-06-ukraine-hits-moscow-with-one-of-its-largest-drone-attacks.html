Ukraine hits Moscow with one of its largest drone attacks

October 06, 2026

Why This Matters

Ukraine launched about 650 drones at Moscow and nearby areas. It killed two people and set the region's largest oil depot on fire. Russia said it stopped most of them, and that night Russia fired 137 drones at Ukraine, with hits recorded in 17 places.

What Others Are Missing

Russia says it shot down 105 drones as they approached the capital, a figure Ukraine has not confirmed. It says most were destroyed far from the city.

What to Watch

Another large strike on Moscow or on Russian oil depots within days would show Ukraine has started a sustained campaign.
