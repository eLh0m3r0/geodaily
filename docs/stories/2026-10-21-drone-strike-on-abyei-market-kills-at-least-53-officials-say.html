Drone strike on Abyei market kills at least 53, officials say

October 21, 2026

Why This Matters

A drone hit Amiet market in Abyei, a region disputed by Sudan and South Sudan, on Monday, starting a fire at a fuel depot. Local officials count 53 to 153 dead and blame Sudan's army; a Sudanese officer said the army carried out the strike.

What Others Are Missing

South Sudan's 2011 independence left Abyei's status unresolved, and the market attack hit civilians in a zone where both armies operate.

What to Watch

A confirmed toll and any Sudanese army statement in 72 hours will show whether the strike becomes a dispute between Khartoum and Juba.
