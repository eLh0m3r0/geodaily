Putin frees US prisoner and agrees to resume Ukraine talks

October 22, 2026

Why This Matters

Vladimir Putin met US envoy Steve Witkoff near St Petersburg and freed Stephen Hubbard, a retired American teacher convicted in Russia. The Kremlin said it is ready to restart three-way talks with Ukraine and the US next month, but Putin still rejected any truce.

What Others Are Missing

Zelensky says Ukraine supports talks between negotiators by early November. Moscow named no date, and Putin dismissed any truce.

What to Watch

Whether Moscow names a date and place for talks between negotiators within 72 hours will show if the promised restart is real.
