Europe's cost-of-living protests turn violent as Italy sends 500 more troops to Milan

October 25, 2026

Why This Matters

Police clashed with demonstrators in France, Italy and Spain over rising living costs and cuts to public services. Italy's premier, Giorgia Meloni, is sending 500 more soldiers to Milan and wants tougher penalties for minors, who police say made up many violent protesters.

What Others Are Missing

Most marchers were peaceful. Sociologist Fabien Truong told Le Monde the violence comes from fragmented, angry youth in working-class neighborhoods, not one organized movement.

What to Watch

Whether other governments copy Italy's troop deployment this week will show if they treat the protests as a policing problem or an economic one.
