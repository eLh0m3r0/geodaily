Congo's ex-president Kabila joins forces with M23 rebels

October 14, 2026

Why This Matters

Joseph Kabila, Congo's ruler for 18 years, has joined a new political and military alliance with the Rwandan-backed M23 rebels. Congo's government in Kinshasa has long accused him of supporting the rebellion. The move threatens last year's peace deal with Rwanda and could widen the war in the east.

What Others Are Missing

Nobody has said what military role Kabila will play. His new group, the FRN, calls itself a national movement demanding a change in how Congo is governed.

What to Watch

Whether the FRN names a military commander in eastern Congo this week will show if Kabila brings fighters or only political influence.
