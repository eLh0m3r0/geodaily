US presses Europe to release diesel reserves as prices climb

October 02, 2026

Why This Matters

The US Treasury told European allies to release emergency diesel supplies immediately to fight soaring prices caused by the war on Iran. EU ministers met Friday to coordinate a response. Diesel runs trucks, farms and factories, so sustained high prices can stoke inflation and slow economies worldwide.

What Others Are Missing

No US release has been announced; Trump only floated the idea. And reserves are buffer stocks, not new supply, so tapping them may calm prices briefly without fixing the shortage.

What to Watch

Whether EU ministers agree on a joint reserve release by Monday. A deal would show Europe yielding to US pressure; failure leaves prices exposed.
