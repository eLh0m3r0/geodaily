Houthis fire a ballistic missile at Riyadh for the first time since the war reignited

September 20, 2026

Why This Matters

Yemen's Houthis tried to hit Riyadh with a ballistic missile, and the Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted the attack. Residents heard an explosion and saw smoke near the airport. This is the first strike on the Saudi capital since fighting reopened, opening a new front in the wider Iran war.

What Others Are Missing

Saudi Arabia confirmed the attack only hours later. Nobody has said what kind of missile it was, where it launched from, or what the intercept damaged.

What to Watch

Watch whether the Saudi-led coalition strikes Houthi launch sites inside Yemen within the next few days.
