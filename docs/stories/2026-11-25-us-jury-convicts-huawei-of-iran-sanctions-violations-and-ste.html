US jury convicts Huawei of Iran sanctions violations and stealing trade secrets

Why This Matters

A New York jury found Huawei guilty of 10 of 12 charges. The charges include lying to banks about its Iran business and plotting to steal US trade secrets. The verdict increases Washington's pressure on China's biggest telecom equipment maker and raises its cost of doing business.
