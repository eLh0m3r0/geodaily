Putin, Xi, and Iran's President Gather in Delhi for BRICS Summit

September 11, 2026

Why This Matters

India is hosting Russia's Putin, China's Xi, and Iran's Pezeshkian for the BRICS summit this weekend. Wars are still happening in Ukraine and the Middle East. This shows unity among countries that disagree with Washington. Modi is balancing ties with Moscow and Beijing. Iran's leader joining shows how much the world's power map is changing.

What Others Are Missing

News shows leader photos but skips real trade or defense deals signed at the summit.

What to Watch

Watch for a joint BRICS statement on Ukraine or Iran, and new India-Russia energy or defense deals.
