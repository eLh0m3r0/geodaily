Pakistan bombs targets in Afghanistan; Taliban says civilians died

September 24, 2026

Why This Matters

Pakistan says it hit 10 militant targets in Afghanistan, including Kandahar. A drone attack had hit a border post first. The Taliban denies involvement and says four civilians died. The two neighbors now trade airstrikes. This helps militants on both sides.

What Others Are Missing

Claims about deaths and injuries and motives come only from the two governments involved. No outside group has checked the claims yet. Each side's account serves its own story.

What to Watch

Watch whether the Taliban strikes back along the border. Also watch whether Pakistan strikes Kandahar again.
