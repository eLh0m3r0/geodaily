NATO says it will defend space after reports of a Russian nuclear-armed satellite

4 outlets · Western media 1 · East Asian media 1 · Russian independent media 1 · Ukrainian media 1

Why This Matters

German media, quoting military and intelligence sources, said Russia may put a nuclear explosive device into orbit within months. NATO chief Mark Rutte said the alliance will defend space, while Russia called the reports baseless. Such a weapon could threaten satellites used by troops, ships and global communications.

What Others Are Missing

The reports do not say whether the satellite is already built. They also do not say when a decision would be made, and Russia denies the claim.

What to Watch

Germany or NATO will publish more evidence from satellite tracking within 72 hours. That release would show whether shared intelligence backs the alarm.
