COP31 draft drops call to phase out fossil fuels

12 reports from 11 outlets · Western media 3 · East Asian media 2 · South Asian media 3 · African media 2 · Global South voices 1 · International organizations 1

Why This Matters

At the COP31 climate summit, negotiators removed a call to end fossil fuels faster. They took it out of a revised draft deal. Countries and activists objected loudly, saying the summit is weakening its core promise, and a final deal is expected on Friday.

What Others Are Missing

The draft still mentions a broad shift in energy. But it no longer names coal, oil and gas, which scientists call the main causes of warming.

What to Watch

A full session on Friday will either adopt the weakened text or send it back. The EU and exposed countries may block a final deal.
