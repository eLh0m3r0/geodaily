Trump says Zelensky won't end the war; Ukraine says US arms are arriving late

October 20, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump wrote that Zelensky 'doesn't want to end this war' and should send someone else to negotiate. He praised Russia's conduct, and Zelensky said US arms shipments are arriving late. Zelensky said Trump's approach amounts to Ukrainian surrender, and their quarrel puts the supply line Kyiv depends on at risk.

What Others Are Missing

Neither side has published the delivery schedule at the dispute's center, so the arms delay is Zelensky's claim, not proven.

What to Watch

Whether a planned US-Ukraine defense meeting goes ahead this week will show if the arms pipeline is freezing; a cancellation would confirm it is.
