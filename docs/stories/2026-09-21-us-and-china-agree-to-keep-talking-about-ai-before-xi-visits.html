US and China agree to keep talking about AI before Xi visits Washington

September 21, 2026

Why This Matters

US and Chinese delegations met in New York Sunday and discussed a formal channel for AI talks. They also worked on trade, with a truce expiring in November. Xi Jinping arrives in Washington this week.

What Others Are Missing

Both sides call the talks 'successful,' but neither described what a joint AI channel would do, who would run it, or how it would be enforced.

What to Watch

Watch for an announced AI working group and any extension of the November tariff truce when Xi lands in Washington.
