Drones hit two cargo ships off Bulgaria, sinking one

October 07, 2026

Why This Matters

Unidentified drones hit two cargo ships in Bulgaria's Black Sea economic zone. The drones hit them on Tuesday, sinking one and leaving its crew missing. Bulgaria is a NATO member, and this is the first drone attack on shipping in its waters. The government has not blamed Moscow, but the opposition says it should.

What Others Are Missing

Bulgaria stopped searching for the missing crew on Wednesday, Vice-Admiral Rumen Nikolov told Nova TV. So the death toll from the sinking is still unknown.

What to Watch

If Bulgaria or NATO names the drone's origin, that will decide if this stays a rescue story or becomes a NATO response.
