Trump announces an 'energy ceasefire' in Ukraine as Kyiv says Russia broke it

October 12, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump said Russia and Ukraine agreed to an immediate 'energy ceasefire'. Zelensky said he first heard of it from Trump's post. Ukraine's foreign minister said Russia hit energy sites hours later. The US had threatened to cut intelligence sharing and arms funding if Kyiv kept hitting Russian refineries.

What Others Are Missing

Moscow has not commented on the ceasefire, and the US threats to Kyiv surfaced only after Trump's diesel deal with Putin.

What to Watch

If Ukrainian drones stay away from Russian refineries for 72 hours, the deal holds; another strike would show it is not.
