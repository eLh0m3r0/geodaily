Canada Hits Back With Tariffs on $20 Billion of US Goods

September 08, 2026

Why This Matters

Canada imposed tariffs up to 50% on US steel, autos and other goods after trade talks collapsed. It's dollar-for-dollar retaliation against Trump's earlier tariffs, hitting swing-state industries before US midterms.

What Others Are Missing

Little attention to how consumers on both sides of the border will feel higher prices first.

What to Watch

Watch for Trump's response or fresh tariff threats within the next few days.
