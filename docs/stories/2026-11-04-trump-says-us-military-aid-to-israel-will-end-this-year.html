Trump says US military aid to Israel will end this year

November 04, 2026

Why This Matters

On Fox News, Trump said he and Netanyahu agreed to stop aid to Israel. The aid is about $4 billion a year and would stop after 2026. The US would still sell Israel weapons, but Trump gave no details. Israeli officials have not publicly confirmed any deal.

What Others Are Missing

Haaretz says the earlier-than-planned cutoff actually fits Netanyahu's goal of a 'Super Sparta' Israel. That Israel would build its own weapons with US partnership.

What to Watch

A public statement from Netanyahu's office within 72 hours will settle the question. It will show whether the deal is real or an election-day remark.
