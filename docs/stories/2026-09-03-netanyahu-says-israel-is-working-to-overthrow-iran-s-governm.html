Netanyahu Says Israel Is Working to Overthrow Iran's Government

September 03, 2026

Why This Matters

Netanyahu says all of Israel's forces are working to remove Iran's government. This is a big shift from just defense to trying to change the regime. He is also trying to unite Israel's far-right before next month's election. Meanwhile, his defense minister says Israel is ready to move Palestinians out of Gaza once the US agrees.

What Others Are Missing

Few reports ask if trying to change Iran's government is legal, or how Israel would actually move over a million people out of Gaza.

What to Watch

Watch for Israeli coalition talks before the election and any US response to the Gaza relocation plan.
