Eritrea cuts all ties with Ethiopia as Tigray war fears grow

October 02, 2026

Why This Matters

Ethiopia ordered its embassy in Eritrea to close and expelled 10 Eritrean diplomats. Asmara responded by severing all diplomatic ties. Overnight blasts were heard in Addis Ababa. The rupture comes as fighting resumes in Tigray, raising the risk of a wider Horn of Africa war.

What Others Are Missing

Each side accuses the other of funding armed groups to topple it, and neither claim is independently verified. Shutting embassies also removes the main channel for quiet talks.

What to Watch

Whether cross-border fighting or fresh talks follow within days. Refugee flows into Sudan or troop moves near the border would show which is coming.
