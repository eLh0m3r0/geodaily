US strikes Iranian air-defence site near Strait of Hormuz

Why This Matters

US forces hit an Iranian air-defence system on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, and Trump called it 'minor'. It was the second such strike this week, days after voters punished Republicans over the war. Iran's state media reported two explosions, and Tehran is considering a mediator's proposal to end the fighting.
