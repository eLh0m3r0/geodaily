Trump-Linked Insiders Cash In as US Takes Control of Venezuela's Oil

September 04, 2026

Why This Matters

A US-backed company now controls Venezuelan oil fields holding 65 billion barrels, locked in for 100 years. Trump appointees and donors are among those securing new deals in the sector. Analysts say the deal won't cut fuel prices despite White House claims.

What Others Are Missing

Little scrutiny yet on how insiders got access to these contracts before the public did.

What to Watch

Watch for the first named companies or contracts tied to Trump-linked figures to surface.
