US launches task force to restrict Iran's land trade

10 reports from 9 outlets · East Asian media 2 · South Asian media 3 · Middle East media 5

Why This Matters

The US military said it is launching Task Force Iron Gate. The force will stop Iran from moving weapons and sanctioned goods across land borders and the Caspian Sea. It builds on a months-long naval blockade and widens pressure on Tehran beyond the Strait of Hormuz.

What Others Are Missing

The task force will be based in Tampa, Florida, with 20 civilian and military staff. Its reach depends on coordination with governments along Iran's borders.

What to Watch

Iran's response at land crossings with Afghanistan, Pakistan and Caspian states will show. The test is whether the new task force can slow weapons flows.
