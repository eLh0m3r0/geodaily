Lula wins Brazil's runoff as his rival refuses to concede

October 26, 2026

Why This Matters

Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva won Brazil's runoff with 51.75% of the vote. Polls had predicted a tight race, but he secured a fourth term. His right-wing rival, Flavio Bolsonaro, refused to concede and said he would ask courts to cancel the result. How Brazil's institutions handle that challenge will shape Latin America's largest democracy.

What Others Are Missing

Brazil's election court has already confirmed the result, so Bolsonaro's call to cancel it has no clear legal path. Lula's win also breaks a rightward trend across Latin America.

What to Watch

Whether Bolsonaro's campaign files a case to cancel the result by midweek, and whether Brazil's armed forces publicly accept Lula's win.
