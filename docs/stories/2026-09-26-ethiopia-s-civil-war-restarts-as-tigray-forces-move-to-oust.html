Ethiopia's Civil War Restarts as Tigray Forces Move to Oust Abiy

September 26, 2026

Why This Matters

The 2022 truce that ended one of this century's deadliest wars has collapsed. Tigray's TPLF says it launched a 'defensive' war to remove Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. The UN rights chief says she is deeply alarmed as fighting spreads.

What Others Are Missing

Most coverage focuses on Tigray alone, but the 2022 deal never settled Amhara and Eritrea disputes, so several conflicts are now merging into one.

What to Watch

Watch whether Eritrean troops or Amhara militias formally join within 72 hours, and whether Addis Ababa's airport stays open.
