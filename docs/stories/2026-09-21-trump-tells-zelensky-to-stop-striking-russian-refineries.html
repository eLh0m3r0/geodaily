Trump tells Zelensky to stop striking Russian refineries

September 21, 2026

Why This Matters

In a phone call Sunday night, Trump asked Ukraine's president to stop hitting Russian oil refineries, saying it is 'all about diesel.' The two leaders will meet in New York on Sept. 22. Ukraine uses those strikes to cut Russia's war money.

What Others Are Missing

Trump wants lower fuel prices. Ukraine's drone strikes on refineries were its best tool for hurting Russia's war economy. Nothing was said about what Ukraine gets back.

What to Watch

Watch whether Ukraine pauses refinery strikes this week and what Trump offers Zelensky at their New York meeting.
