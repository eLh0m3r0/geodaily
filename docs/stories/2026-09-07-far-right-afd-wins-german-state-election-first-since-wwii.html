Far-right AfD wins German state election, first since WWII

September 07, 2026

Why This Matters

The AfD won over 44% of the vote in Saxony-Anhalt. It could soon lead a German state government for the first time since the Nazi era. Sweden's center-left leader also warned that Russia is now shaping her country's election. Together, these show far-right, Russia-linked politics gaining real power in Europe.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on the shock win. It skips whether other parties can form a coalition to block AfD.

What to Watch

Watch if mainstream parties in Saxony-Anhalt can team up to keep AfD out of power.
