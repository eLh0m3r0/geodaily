Trump agrees the Iran war cost Republicans the midterms

November 05, 2026

Why This Matters

Vice President JD Vance said the Iran war was 'the reason we lost.' Trump called that 'largely true.' Democrats will control both chambers in January. That lets them block war spending and call officials to testify. A president who says he would do it again now faces a Congress that can slow him.

What Others Are Missing

Trump linked the loss to higher gasoline prices. He said he would repeat the war because 'long-term, it's the only answer.' Dawn reported Vance is distancing himself ahead of a possible 2028 run.

What to Watch

Whether Democratic leaders schedule an Iran war funding vote or hearing within days will show how fast Congress moves to limit him.
