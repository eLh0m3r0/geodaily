Russia-Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi make little progress

Why This Matters

Russia, Ukraine and the US met in Abu Dhabi for their first three-way talks in about nine months. Day one focused on safety pledges for Kyiv, but Russia pushed a broader safety deal for Europe after the war. Russia gave little on a ceasefire, while the US called the meeting a step that builds momentum; Zelensky called it constructive.
