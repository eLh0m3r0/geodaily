Trump rules out attacking Iran before November midterms

October 09, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump wrote on Truth Social that the US will not attack Iran before the November 3 midterms. He said talks with Tehran are productive, but Iran will never get a nuclear weapon, and oil prices had risen on fears of new strikes.

What Others Are Missing

Trump tied a decision about national security to an election date, an unusually public link. Iran has so far not confirmed the 'productive discussions' he described.

What to Watch

If Tehran confirms direct talks with Washington, that would show the pause is diplomacy and not just election timing.
