Houthis claim deadly strikes on Saudi airports

October 08, 2026

Why This Matters

Houthi attacks on Saudi airports killed an Egyptian, an Algerian, and a Sudanese national and wounded 36 others. The strike on Riyadh's main airport is a sharp escalation in the Yemen war. It directly tests Saudi Arabia's new defense pact with Pakistan and Turkey.

What Others Are Missing

The Houthis also said they hit a military convoy near Aden's airport. The Saudi-led coalition says it destroyed 82 Houthi targets, but independent checks have not verified either claim.

What to Watch

If Riyadh bombs Houthi-held Sanaa or Yemen's ports within three days, the war is widening; a new truce effort would show restraint.
