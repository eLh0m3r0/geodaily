Hackers breach US Air Force Cloud One platform

12 reports from 11 outlets · Western media 1 · East Asian media 4 · South Asian media 5 · Middle East media 1 · Latin American media 1

Why This Matters

Hackers reportedly broke into Cloud One, the Air Force's main cloud system, and reached air-defense radar and munitions tracking data. The Pentagon says its networks are secure but did not deny the breach, and that raises questions about US military cyber defenses.

What Others Are Missing

Cloud One gathers data from more than 200 Air Force sites worldwide. The reported breach could expose supply and personnel records, not just radar feeds.

What to Watch

See whether the Pentagon confirms the breach or names the hackers, since formal naming would turn this into a major cyber-diplomacy fight.
