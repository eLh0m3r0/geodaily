Trump signs Russia sanctions bill, letting him hit China and India with 100% tariffs

September 19, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump signed a law letting him impose tariffs up to 100% on countries that buy Russian oil and gas. That mainly means China and India. The law also hits Russia's energy and defense firms and its shadow tanker fleet.

What Others Are Missing

The law gives Trump sweeping tariff power with little oversight; Democratic lawmakers flagged that. It is still unclear if he will actually target India or China.

What to Watch

Watch whether Trump names specific countries for tariffs within days, and how Delhi and Beijing respond.
