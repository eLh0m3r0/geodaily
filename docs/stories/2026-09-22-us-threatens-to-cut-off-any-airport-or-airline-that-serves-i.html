US threatens to cut off any airport or airline that serves Iran

September 22, 2026

Why This Matters

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent says Iran's airlines will be shut down worldwide within days. Any airport or company that fuels or services them could lose access to the dollar system. China buys most of Iran's oil and calls the move illegal.

What Others Are Missing

Most coverage frames this as an Iran story. The real target is Chinese refiners, shippers and banks. Washington must punish China or back down.

What to Watch

Watch whether any airline or airport stops serving Iran by September 23, and whether Chinese refiners keep buying Iranian crude.
