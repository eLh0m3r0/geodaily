Russian drone strike on Dnipro train station kills at least 13

October 30, 2026

Why This Matters

A Russian drone hit a Dnipro railway station, killing at least 13 people as a train waited there. The EU’s top diplomat condemned the attack and called for those responsible to face a special tribunal.

What Others Are Missing

UN officials said 10 people were injured, while local reports gave a lower figure; casualty counts in strikes often shift.

What to Watch

Ukraine’s air force should report within 72 hours whether Russia used a new drone model or tactic at Dnipro, which would shape city defenses.
