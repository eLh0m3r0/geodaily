Aramco building hit as Saudi Arabia and Houthis trade strikes

October 14, 2026

Why This Matters

An Aramco office building in Dhahran caught fire late Tuesday, and Saudi Arabia blamed Houthi missiles. Saudi jets hit fuel and energy sites in Yemen in return. Debris from missiles shot down over Riyadh hurt four people, and the exchange puts Gulf oil sites and airports at risk.

What Others Are Missing

Saudi Arabia has not shown public proof linking the Dhahran fire to a specific launch. The Houthis now threaten Saudi airports and refineries by name.

What to Watch

If Saudi jets hit Yemen's ports again within 72 hours, that means Riyadh is targeting Houthi missile supplies, not only hitting back.
