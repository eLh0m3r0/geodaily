Alberta votes on separation as Canada's PM says the US wants to break the country

October 16, 2026

Why This Matters

Alberta, Canada's oil heartland, votes October 19 on whether to start the legal path toward leaving Canada. Prime Minister Mark Carney said outside forces are working to break the country, an implied accusation against the United States. A strong separatist result would shake a G7 ally and North American energy trade.

What Others Are Missing

Far-right populism, conspiracy theories and a feeling of distance from Ottawa drive the push, political scientist Jared Wesley says. The referendum asks about starting a process, not immediate independence.

What to Watch

If the separatist side tops 40 percent, Carney will face pressure to offer Alberta a new energy and budget deal.
