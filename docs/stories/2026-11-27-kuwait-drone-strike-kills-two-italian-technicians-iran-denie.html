Kuwait drone strike kills two Italian technicians; Iran denies role

Why This Matters

A drone attack on a Kuwaiti air-defence site killed two Italian technicians and injured Kuwaiti and Pakistani workers. Italy and Kuwait blamed Iran, which denied involvement. It is the first fatal Iranian attack on Kuwaiti soil since July and raises the risk of a wider Gulf war.
