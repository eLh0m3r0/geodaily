Xi Jinping's Washington state visit starts Wednesday

September 22, 2026

Why This Matters

Xi Jinping arrives Wednesday for his second meeting with Trump in six months. Washington plans a rare airport welcome. Both sides call ties stable, but pressure is building under the surface.

What Others Are Missing

Japan-China ties are worsening fast, and Europe has no seat at this table. Both will live with whatever the two presidents decide.

What to Watch

Watch for any announced deal on tariffs, AI rules or Taiwan language during the September 23-25 visit.
