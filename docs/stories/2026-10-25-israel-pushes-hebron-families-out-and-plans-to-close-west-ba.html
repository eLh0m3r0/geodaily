Israel pushes Hebron families out and plans to close West Bank crossings on election day

October 25, 2026

Why This Matters

Israel's army said it will close every land crossing with the West Bank on Tuesday's election day, barring Palestinian workers. Troops have pushed about 2,000 Hebron residents from their homes after gunmen killed two soldiers. Defense Minister Israel Katz said they will not return until Israel finishes dealing with Hamas.

What Others Are Missing

Counts differ: Palestinian officials cite more than 2,200 displaced, while Israeli and wire reports say about 250 families. Katz gave no date for anyone's return.

What to Watch

Whether Hebron residents are allowed home after Tuesday's vote will show if the operation was shaped by election timing.
