ICJ finds Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya

Why This Matters

The International Court of Justice ruled that Myanmar broke the Genocide Convention in its 2017 attacks on Rohingya Muslims. This is the first time the court has found a state at fault for genocide. The ruling could shape South Africa's genocide case against Israel.
