Israel's parliament elects Netanyahu opponent as speaker

20 reports from 12 outlets · Western media 6 · East Asian media 2 · South Asian media 2 · Middle East media 9 · African media 1

Why This Matters

Israeli lawmakers elected Matan Kahana, an ally of Gadi Eisenkot, as speaker with 66 votes to Likud's 52. The vote weakens caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu as Eisenkot tries to form a government. Police and Shin Bet face claims they helped Netanyahu challenge election results.

What Others Are Missing

Le Monde reports that no evidence has emerged for Netanyahu's claims of 'massive fraud' in Arab lists. Even so, police raided and detained people over them.

What to Watch

Eisenkot will announce a coalition within 72 hours or ask for more time; failure would raise the chance of another Israeli election.
