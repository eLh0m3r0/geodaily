Russia hits Kyiv's Dnipro bridges for the first time

October 04, 2026

Why This Matters

Russian drones and missiles damaged Kyiv's Northern Bridge over the Dnipro and killed six people elsewhere in Ukraine. The war had left the bridges alone for nearly four years. The strike suggests Moscow wants to make the capital harder to live in and harder to supply as winter nears.

What Others Are Missing

Kyiv says it has documents showing Putin plans to hit more civilian targets before winter. Zelensky has promised more Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil refineries.

What to Watch

Another strike on a Dnipro crossing or a Kyiv power plant within 72 hours would show whether this is a campaign or a one-off.
