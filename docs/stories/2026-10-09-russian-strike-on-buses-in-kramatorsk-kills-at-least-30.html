Russian strike on buses in Kramatorsk kills at least 30

October 09, 2026

Why This Matters

A guided bomb exploded beside public buses in frontline Kramatorsk, killing at least 30 people in one of the deadliest strikes in weeks. Public transport shut down, and Russian attacks worsened blackouts in Kyiv.

What Others Are Missing

The strike landed hours before US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner were due to meet Ukrainian and European officials in Miami. They were due to bring new proposals to end the war.

What to Watch

If Ukraine confirms the toll reached 33, this would become the deadliest single strike in weeks.
