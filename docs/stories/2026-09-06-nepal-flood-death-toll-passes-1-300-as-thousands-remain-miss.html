Nepal Flood Death Toll Passes 1,300 as Thousands Remain Missing

September 06, 2026

Why This Matters

Nepal's death toll from flash floods has climbed to 1,344, with about 5,500 people still missing ten days on. Rescuers, including Chinese and South Korean teams, are still pulling survivors from collapsed tunnels and buried homes.

What Others Are Missing

Rescue stories overshadow questions about why hydropower and road infrastructure failed so badly in the flood zone.

What to Watch

Watch for the missing count to shrink or grow sharply as search teams reach remote valleys this week.
