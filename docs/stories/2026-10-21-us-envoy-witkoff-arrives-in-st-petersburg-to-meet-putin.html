US envoy Witkoff arrives in St Petersburg to meet Putin

October 21, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump's envoy Steve Witkoff arrived in St Petersburg for talks with Vladimir Putin on the war. European leaders are debating their own talks with the Kremlin; the Financial Times reports one idea: Russia keeps the Donbas and Ukraine gets NATO membership. Trump again said Ukraine must want a deal.

What Others Are Missing

The idea that Russia keeps the Donbas and Ukraine joins NATO comes from a Financial Times columnist. That writer described what European politicians now say privately, but no government has confirmed it as policy.

What to Watch

A joint statement or a follow-up meeting after Witkoff's St Petersburg talks would show whether Washington is negotiating terms or only listening.
