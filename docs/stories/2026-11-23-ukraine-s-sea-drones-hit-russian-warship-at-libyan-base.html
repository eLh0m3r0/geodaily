Ukraine's sea drones hit Russian warship at Libyan base

Why This Matters

Ukrainian sea drones hit the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi in eastern Libya. The attack badly damaged the ship and set the base on fire. Zelensky confirmed the attack and said it also hit a cargo ship carrying weapons. Ukraine has now taken its war against Russia's fleet into the Mediterranean.
