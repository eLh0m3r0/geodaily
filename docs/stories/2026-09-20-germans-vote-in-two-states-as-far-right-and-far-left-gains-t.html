Germans vote in two states as far-right and far-left gains threaten Chancellor Merz

September 20, 2026

Why This Matters

About 3.8 million voters in Berlin and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania are choosing state parliaments. Polls show the far-right AfD and the radical left gaining. A bad result would further weaken Chancellor Friedrich Merz, whose support was already wobbling after a defeat two weeks ago.

What Others Are Missing

These are state votes, not national ones. But they are the clearest test yet of whether Merz's coalition can survive the year.

What to Watch

Watch the AfD's vote share in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania and whether CDU lawmakers start questioning Merz openly.
