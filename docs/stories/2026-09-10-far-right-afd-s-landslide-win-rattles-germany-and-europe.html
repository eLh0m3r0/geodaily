Far-Right AfD's Landslide Win Rattles Germany and Europe

September 10, 2026

Why This Matters

The AfD nearly doubled its 2021 result to take 43.8% in Saxony-Anhalt. The win pressures Chancellor Merz and could push Berlin toward a softer line on China and looser EU ties.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage dwells on shockwaves in Berlin and Paris but says little about the economic grievances driving voters to the AfD.

What to Watch

Watch for Merz's coalition to debate migration and China policy within days.
