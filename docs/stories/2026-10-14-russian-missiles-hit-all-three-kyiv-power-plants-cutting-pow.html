Russian missiles hit all three Kyiv power plants, cutting power to 600,000

October 14, 2026

Why This Matters

Ballistic missiles hit all three Kyiv plants that make heat and power, plus other key facilities in the region. The strikes killed at least three people and hurt 22, and more than 600,000 lost electricity. They came days after Trump announced a ceasefire on energy targets that Zelensky says Russia is breaking.

What Others Are Missing

Zelensky said allies gave no warning before the strike, suggesting the sharing of intelligence to protect Ukrainian cities has shrunk.

What to Watch

If Russia hits Kyiv's remaining substations again this week, the announced ceasefire on energy targets will be effectively dead.
