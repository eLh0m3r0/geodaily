Zelensky Warns Airlines Russian Skies Are No Longer Safe

September 03, 2026

Why This Matters

Zelensky says more Ukrainian drone strikes have basically shut down Russian airspace for civilian flights. Ukraine has hit oil terminals, refineries, and air bases deep inside Russia. The UN's aviation agency is now warning airlines to be careful.

What Others Are Missing

Few reports look at how these strikes on refineries and oil terminals could raise global oil prices or squeeze supply.

What to Watch

Watch for airlines to change flight routes or for a formal safety warning from the UN aviation agency soon.
