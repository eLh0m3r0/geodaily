Nigeria records first Ebola case as outbreak spreads

October 21, 2026

Why This Matters

Nigeria confirmed its first Ebola case on Tuesday, in a patient who arrived from Uganda, and Kenya declared a ninth case. The Democratic Republic of Congo declared an Ebola outbreak in May, and it has now spread across several countries. Nigeria's size and travel links make it harder to contain the virus.

What Others Are Missing

Uganda's health ministry said it has no active Ebola cases, even as its neighbor confirmed an infection in a traveler who came from there.

What to Watch

Whether Nigeria reports more cases by Friday will show if the virus is spreading inside Africa's most populous country or border officials caught it.
