Saudi Arabia plans offensive to break Houthi grip on Red Sea shipping

October 03, 2026

Why This Matters

Saudi Arabia is preparing an offensive in Yemen within weeks. It aims to break the Houthi grip on Red Sea shipping, officials told Reuters. Yemeni ground forces will lead it, with Saudi air cover. Pakistan may take over defensive duties, and fighting already killed 80 combatants in one day.

What Others Are Missing

Neither report explains what would make this campaign different from the decade-long Saudi-led war that failed to remove the Houthis.

What to Watch

Whether Saudi airstrikes actually begin within weeks; if so, the Yemen war widens while US-Iran tensions are high.
