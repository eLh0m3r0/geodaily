Moscow says Trump's diesel waiver will send Russian fuel to the US

October 15, 2026

Why This Matters

President Trump suspended the ban on Russian diesel exports last week. On Wednesday, Russia's deputy prime minister, Alexander Novak, said direct shipments to the United States will follow, starting with more than 300,000 tonnes. The waiver conflicts with a 2022 law Congress passed to ban Russian fuel imports.

What Others Are Missing

Foreign Policy notes the deal has no clear legal basis. Trump signed tougher sanctions on Russian energy less than a month before offering the waiver.

What to Watch

A court or Congress could challenge the waiver within days; a legal filing would show whether the shipments can legally begin.
