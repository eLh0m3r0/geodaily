Iran war costs near $40 billion as US munitions run short

September 17, 2026

Why This Matters

A congressional report puts the Iran war's cost near $40 billion through August. The Pentagon's inspector general admits munitions stockpiles are depleted. The war is unpopular as midterm elections approach.

What Others Are Missing

The CBO's $38 billion figure is far below the $100 billion independent experts estimate. The gap may hide in future budgets.

What to Watch

Watch for a supplemental defense spending request or a public fight over the real war cost.
