Nepal-Tibet Flood Death Toll Nearly Doubles, 3,000 Still Missing

August 30, 2026

Why This Matters

The confirmed death toll jumped from around 359 to at least 682 in just days. Nepal now says 3,000 people are still missing, more than double earlier estimates. Rescuers are also racing to save hydropower workers trapped in flooded tunnels as a landslide dam threatens fresh flooding.

What Others Are Missing

Little coverage explains why an upstream landslide dam could trigger a second wave of flooding before rescue teams finish their work.

What to Watch

Watch for whether the landslide dam breaches, which could send a new flood surge downstream within days.
