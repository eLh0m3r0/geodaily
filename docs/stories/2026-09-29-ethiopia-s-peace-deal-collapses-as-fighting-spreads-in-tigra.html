Ethiopia's peace deal collapses as fighting spreads in Tigray

September 29, 2026

Why This Matters

Ethiopia's military and rebel forces are fighting in Tigray, Amhara, and Afar. A four-year-old peace deal has collapsed. The government's large drone fleet gives it air power. This raises fears of a wider war in the Horn of Africa.

What Others Are Missing

The 2022 peace deal ended one of Africa's deadliest wars. But its collapse has not drawn the same global attention.

What to Watch

Watch whether federal forces push toward Mekelle or rebels open new fronts in Amhara.
