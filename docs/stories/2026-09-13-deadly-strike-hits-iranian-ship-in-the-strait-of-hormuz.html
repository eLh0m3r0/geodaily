Deadly Strike Hits Iranian Ship in the Strait of Hormuz

September 13, 2026

Why This Matters

A strike killed one crew member and wounded three others on an Iranian cargo ship near Qeshm Island. It happened a day before Iran was set to brief Gulf states in Oman on protecting shipping. The strait carries a fifth of the world's oil, and repeated attacks there are already pushing energy prices up.

What Others Are Missing

No outlet has independently confirmed who fired the shot. Iranian state media is the only source, and it just blames a 'terrorist enemy.'

What to Watch

Watch whether Gulf states name a culprit at Monday's Oman meeting on shipping safety.
