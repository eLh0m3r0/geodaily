Bomb threats target five European flights; no explosives found

November 04, 2026

Why This Matters

Reuters and DW reported that five airliners received bomb threats the same day. The five threatened airliners were flying to Warsaw, Rome, Sofia and Spain. Every plane landed safely and searches found nothing, but the timing forced disruptions across several European airports.

What Others Are Missing

No group has claimed the threats, and no arrests were reported. So it remains unknown whether this was one campaign or a copycat hoax wave.

What to Watch

Polish and Italian police will say within days whether one source sent the threats. Any arrests would point to an organized campaign.
