Putin Halts Kyiv Strikes for 72 Hours as US Envoys Push Peace Talks

September 06, 2026

Why This Matters

Putin ordered a three-day pause on strikes against Kyiv as US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner visited Moscow and Kyiv. It's the clearest sign yet the US push for peace talks has some traction, even as Zelensky's political support wavers at home.

What Others Are Missing

The pause only covers Kyiv; fighting continues elsewhere in Ukraine, and past pauses have collapsed quickly.

What to Watch

Watch if the pause holds past September 8 and whether Kushner and Witkoff secure a wider truce.
