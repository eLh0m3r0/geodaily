Nvidia probes whether CEO Jensen Huang was target of foreign intelligence

November 09, 2026

Why This Matters

Nvidia's board is investigating whether CEO Jensen Huang was the target of foreign spying. The inquiry follows his admission of an affair with a Chinese businesswoman. Nvidia makes the chips at the center of the US-China AI race. Any security breach would matter far beyond the company.

What Others Are Missing

Huang apologized and said he will keep leading Nvidia. No US agency has confirmed the spying allegation, which Nvidia is still investigating.

What to Watch

The question is whether Nvidia names outside investigators or US officials open a security review within 72 hours.
