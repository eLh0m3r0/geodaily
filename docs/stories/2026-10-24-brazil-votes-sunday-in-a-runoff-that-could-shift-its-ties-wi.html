Brazil votes Sunday in a runoff that could shift its ties with the US and China

October 24, 2026

Why This Matters

Brazil votes Sunday in a runoff between Lula and Sen. Flávio Bolsonaro. A campaign-finance scandal has cut Flávio's poll numbers, and the winner will shape how Brazil sells the rare earths the West wants for its break with China.

What Others Are Missing

The disputed funds were meant to finance a biopic of Jair Bolsonaro, who is under house arrest for plotting a coup after the 2022 election.

What to Watch

Whether Flávio keeps sliding in Sunday's count will show if the scandal, rather than Lula's record, decided the race.
