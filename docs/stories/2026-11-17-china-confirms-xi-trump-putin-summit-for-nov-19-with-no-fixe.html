China confirms Xi-Trump-Putin summit for Nov 19 with no fixed agenda

11 reports from 6 outlets · Western media 1 · East Asian media 1 · South Asian media 1 · Russian state media 6 · Russian independent media 1 · African media 1

Why This Matters

China said the first three-way summit between Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin will be in Shenzhen on November 19. The meeting has no pre-agreed agenda, an unusual step for Beijing. It could reshape how the three nuclear powers handle Ukraine, trade and security.

What Others Are Missing

The Financial Times reports no pre-agreed agenda for the meeting. Beijing usually insists on strict formal rules, so the format itself is a concession.

What to Watch

Whether all three leaders appear together on November 19, rather than sending deputies, will show if the summit is real.
