Iran Says It Hit a UAE Air Base; UAE Says That Never Happened

August 31, 2026

Why This Matters

Iran's army says it fired drones at a UAE air base early Monday. The UAE calls that false. The dispute comes right after US strikes on Iranian rocket launchers, showing the war could pull in more Gulf states.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage rarely mentions Al-Minhad hosts US troops, so any real strike there risks direct US-Iran combat.

What to Watch

Watch for UAE or US confirmation, satellite proof, or new Iranian strikes within 72 hours.
