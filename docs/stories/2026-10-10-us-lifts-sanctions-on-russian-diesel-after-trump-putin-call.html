US lifts sanctions on Russian diesel after Trump-Putin call

October 10, 2026

Why This Matters

The US Treasury lifted sanctions on Russian diesel minutes after Trump and Putin spoke by phone. Trump said Russia will supply American and global markets. Ukraine's president said his country is 'being used as a front' by the deal. It eases pressure on Moscow's energy earnings while the war continues.

What Others Are Missing

The Kremlin said the call also covered Iran and the war in Ukraine. Russia still occupies about one-fifth of Ukrainian territory.

What to Watch

Whether the Miami talks produce a written ceasefire proposal or Ukraine walks out over the diesel deal will show if Washington still coordinates with Kyiv.
