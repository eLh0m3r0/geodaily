Paraguay's bank collapse freezes savings and fuels impeachment calls

November 05, 2026

Why This Matters

Paraguay's central bank took over Ueno Bank overnight, leaving millions of customers unable to reach their accounts. The Senate voted to let savers take out up to 40 minimum wages within 24 hours. Opposition lawmakers are now pushing to impeach President Santiago Peña.

What Others Are Missing

The payment plan still needs the lower house, which was due to consider it on Thursday. The early payment is capped at about 121 million guaraníes per saver.

What to Watch

Whether the Chamber of Deputies passes the payment bill Thursday and money reaches savers within 24 hours will decide whether protests fade.
