US military weighs 'mother of all bombs' for strikes on Iran, CNN reports

October 15, 2026

Why This Matters

CNN reported Thursday that US military planners have drawn up options against Iranian energy and nuclear sites. Those options include the GBU-43/B, the largest non-nuclear bomb in the US arsenal. Trump would have to order renewed combat for any strike to happen.

What Others Are Missing

The options come from unnamed US officials, and no order has been announced. It is unclear whether this is a real plan or a message to Tehran.

What to Watch

Whether Trump repeats the threat or US bombers reposition in the next few days will show if the plan is more than a message.
