Tigray forces seize Mekelle airport, reviving Ethiopia's civil war fears

September 24, 2026

Why This Matters

Tigrayan forces took Mekelle airport this week. Days earlier, former battlefield enemies joined forces against Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed. The 2022 peace deal ended a war that killed hundreds of thousands. That deal is now breaking down. New fighting would make the Horn of Africa less stable.

What Others Are Missing

Stories focus on the airport. The real shift is a new alliance of former enemies against Abiy. This coalition crosses old ethnic lines.

What to Watch

Watch whether federal troops advance on Mekelle. Also watch whether Eritrea opens a new front within 72 hours.
