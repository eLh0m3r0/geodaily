G7 agrees to release 100 million barrels of emergency fuel

October 03, 2026

Why This Matters

The G7 will release 100 million barrels of diesel and crude from emergency stockpiles. The release will take four months, with a large diesel release in the first 20 days. That is roughly one day of global oil demand. President Trump dropped a threatened ban on US diesel exports after the deal.

What Others Are Missing

Foreign Policy reports crude is moving out of Hormuz again. But diesel and other refined fuels are not leaving Russia or China, so pump relief may take months.

What to Watch

Whether the large early diesel release actually ships within 20 days; if not, pump prices keep climbing.
