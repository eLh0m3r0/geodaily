Germany arrests its former spy chief on treason charges

October 07, 2026

Why This Matters

German prosecutors detained August Hanning on suspicion of passing state secrets to a foreign intelligence service. He led the BND from 1998 to 2005. His former bureau chief was arrested with him. A former top spy facing treason charges is rare and damaging for Germany's security services.

What Others Are Missing

German media say the foreign service was not Russian. Haaretz reports the evidence came from a Danish investigation into an heiress's abducted children that involved former Israeli agents.

What to Watch

Prosecutors must soon decide whether to formally charge Hanning or release him. A charge sheet would name the foreign intelligence service.
