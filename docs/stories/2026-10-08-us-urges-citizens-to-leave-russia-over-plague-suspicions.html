US urges citizens to leave Russia over plague suspicions

October 08, 2026

Why This Matters

A Siberian lab worker died of a suspected plague infection, and Russia now reports a second suspected case. The US embassy told Americans to consider leaving, the WHO says it still lacks details, and Mongolia has tightened border checks.

What Others Are Missing

Russia calls reports of a second case 'false information,' and the WHO says no cases have been recorded in Irkutsk. So the size of any outbreak is still unconfirmed.

What to Watch

If Moscow names a lab-confirmed cause within 72 hours, the mystery would settle. If more countries issue travel advice, that would mean wider alarm.
