Trump says the US war in Iran is 'militarily effectively over'

Why This Matters

President Trump said Monday the US bombing campaign in Iran is 'militarily effectively over.' He suggested he will not resume strikes for now. After meeting Qatar's prime minister, he said Tehran was 'talking very politely.' He warned US forces remain 'locked and loaded.' The shift matters for oil markets, Gulf security and the war's civilian toll.
