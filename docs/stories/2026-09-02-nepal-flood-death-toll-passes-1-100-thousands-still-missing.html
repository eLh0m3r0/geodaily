Nepal Flood Death Toll Passes 1,100, Thousands Still Missing

September 02, 2026

Why This Matters

Nepal's flash flood death toll has risen to 1,127. Nearly 4,000 people are still missing. Rescuers are still searching collapsed hydropower sites a week later. This is now one of the deadliest disasters in Nepal's history.

What Others Are Missing

News focuses on the death count. It gives less attention to trapped hydropower workers and health risks facing women and girls, as the UN warns.

What to Watch

Expect the confirmed death toll to keep rising as officials use DNA tests to identify unnamed bodies.
