Brazil's election goes to a runoff as Bolsonaro edges past Lula

October 05, 2026

Why This Matters

Flávio Bolsonaro won 47.1% of the vote to Lula's 45% in Sunday's first round. They face a runoff on October 25, and if Bolsonaro wins, Brazil becomes the latest Latin American country to swing right. That would have big effects for the Amazon and regional alliances.

What Others Are Missing

The first round also showed strong support for Bolsonaro's far-right movement. It has not promised to accept a runoff defeat, so some fear a repeat of the 2023 coup attempt.

What to Watch

Lula and Bolsonaro will begin televised debates next week. Whether Lula can win back centrist support will decide the runoff.
