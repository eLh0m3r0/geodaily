Houthis Seize Key Red Sea Islands, Squeezing a Vital Shipping Route

September 15, 2026

Why This Matters

Iran-backed Houthi fighters took two more islands near the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Ships carrying oil and cargo must pass through this narrow route. Saudi Arabia depends on it for exports. This week's attacks near Hormuz add more risk. Shippers and insurers may start avoiding the region. That could raise costs for goods worldwide.

What Others Are Missing

Reports don't say how closely Iran is coordinating this, or who controls the islands day to day.

What to Watch

Watch for a Saudi military response or higher shipping insurance rates for Red Sea routes within 72 hours.
