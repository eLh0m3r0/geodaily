US Plans $2.8 Billion Bomb Sale to Israel, Including 40,000 Heavy Bombs

September 16, 2026

Why This Matters

The US told Congress about a deal to send Israel 40,000 more MK-84 bombs. Each bomb weighs 2,000 pounds. This sale would help Israel strike targets linked to Iran more easily. It comes as Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and Mecca push the region closer to a bigger war.

What Others Are Missing

Most reports rely on unnamed officials and one Washington Post story. Israel has barely confirmed this.

What to Watch

Expect a Congressional vote or challenge to this sale within a week.
