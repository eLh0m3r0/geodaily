EU proposes new Russia sanctions, including SWIFT ban for 60 banks

4 outlets · Western media 1 · East Asian media 1 · Russian state media 1 · Russian independent media 1

Why This Matters

The European Commission proposed its 22nd set of Russia sanctions, including removing 60 banks from SWIFT. It targets crypto and payment networks, energy services and other countries that help Russia avoid sanctions. It would also deny visas to Russians who fought in Ukraine and ban Russian athletes from European events.

What Others Are Missing

The package is only a proposal; all 27 EU governments must approve it, and talks could weaken some measures.

What to Watch

EU governments will debate the package within 72 hours. Objections from any member state would show which parts governments may weaken.
