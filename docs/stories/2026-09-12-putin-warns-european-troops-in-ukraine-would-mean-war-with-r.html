Putin Warns European Troops in Ukraine Would Mean War With Russia

September 12, 2026

Why This Matters

Putin said sending European troops to Ukraine would count as war against Russia. He claimed Europe made up a fake threat from Moscow. This warning comes as some European governments consider sending forces to back a future ceasefire.

What Others Are Missing

State media leaves out that Europe's troop talk is a direct response to Russia's own military buildup near NATO borders.

What to Watch

Watch whether France, the UK, or other European states announce or drop Ukraine deployment plans this week.
