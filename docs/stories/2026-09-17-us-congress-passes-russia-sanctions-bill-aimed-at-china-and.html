US Congress passes Russia sanctions bill aimed at China and India

September 17, 2026

Why This Matters

Congress passed a bill to punish Russia's energy and defense industries, plus the tanker 'shadow fleet' that dodges sanctions. It also threatens 100% tariffs on countries buying Russian oil, including China and India. The vote lands days before Xi Jinping visits Washington.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on Moscow, but the sharpest pain may fall on Indian and Chinese refiners. Trump can still soften the law before or after signing it.

What to Watch

Watch whether Trump signs it unchanged or waters it down before Xi's Washington visit.
