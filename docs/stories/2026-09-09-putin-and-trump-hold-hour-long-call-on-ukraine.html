Putin and Trump Hold Hour-Long Call on Ukraine

September 09, 2026

Why This Matters

Putin and Trump spoke for an hour after US envoys Witkoff and Kushner visited Moscow and Kyiv. Both sides called it constructive, but no deal was announced. This is the most direct US-Russia contact since the failed peace push.

What Others Are Missing

The Kremlin readout gives few real details on concessions, and it's unclear Kyiv was consulted before the call.

What to Watch

Watch for an announcement of a Trump-Putin or Putin-Zelensky summit within a week.
