Trump says a Putin-Zelensky meeting will be set up 'very soon'

October 29, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump said a meeting between Putin and Zelensky would be arranged 'very soon' to end the war, now in its fifth year. No date or venue has been given, and Zelensky is uneasy about a separate US-Russia-China summit planned for next month.

What Others Are Missing

The Kremlin has not confirmed it will attend any Putin-Zelensky meeting; for now, the claim rests on Trump alone.

What to Watch

A Kremlin statement on whether Putin will meet Zelensky, or a White House date for the US-Russia-China summit, would show if this is real.
