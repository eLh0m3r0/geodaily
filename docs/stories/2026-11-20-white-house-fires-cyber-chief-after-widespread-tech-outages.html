White House fires cyber chief after widespread tech outages

17 reports from 14 outlets · Western media 4 · East Asian media 2 · South Asian media 8 · Middle East media 1 · Russian state media 1 · African media 1

Why This Matters

President Trump removed National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross after a week of outages that disrupted air travel, banking and classified messages. The White House named a Defense Department official to the post on a temporary basis. Lawmakers are demanding answers about the outages that disrupted air travel and banking.

What Others Are Missing

The outages hit Microsoft users worldwide and halted US air travel. The government has not published a timeline of what failed.

What to Watch

Senate committees may call Cairncross or the temporary director to testify. They may also demand a public timeline of the outages.
