Ukraine resumes refinery strikes after Zelensky says the pause is over

October 15, 2026

Why This Matters

Zelensky told the Financial Times that Ukraine is done holding back on Russian oil refineries. A strike in Russia's Ryazan region followed on Thursday. Refinery attacks are how Kyiv squeezes the oil revenue that funds Russia's war.

What Others Are Missing

Zelensky also said in the interview that the US has reduced intelligence sharing with Kyiv, which he called dangerous for Ukraine's defenses.

What to Watch

Whether Ukraine hits more refineries in the next 72 hours will show if Trump's energy ceasefire has effectively collapsed.
