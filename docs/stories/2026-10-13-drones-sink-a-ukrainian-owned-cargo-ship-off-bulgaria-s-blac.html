Drones sink a Ukrainian-owned cargo ship off Bulgaria's Black Sea coast

October 13, 2026

Why This Matters

Strikes hit two cargo ships overnight off Bulgaria, a NATO member, and one, the Ukrainian-owned Chelsea 3, sank. Bulgaria's prime minister called the attack unacceptable and said Sofia plans to call in Russia's ambassador after the second such strike in a week.

What Others Are Missing

No one has claimed the attack, and Bulgaria has not publicly named who launched the drones. Rescuers saved one person from the sunken ship and took 15 off the other.

What to Watch

Whether Sofia publicly blames Moscow, and whether NATO takes up the sinking, will show how far the war is spreading.
