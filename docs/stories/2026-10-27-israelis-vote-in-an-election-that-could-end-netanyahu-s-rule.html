Israelis vote in an election that could end Netanyahu's rule

October 27, 2026

Why This Matters

Israelis voted Tuesday for the first time since the October 7, 2023 attack. Polls suggest Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's coalition may lose its majority to former army chief Gadi Eisenkot's bloc. The result will shape the wars in Gaza and Iran and decide whether Israel's far-right partners keep power.

What Others Are Missing

Arab parties are projected to decide who governs. A US nonprofit tracking the election said tens of thousands of Israelis living abroad flew home to vote.

What to Watch

Results and coalition numbers should arrive within days. If Eisenkot's bloc reaches 61 seats, Netanyahu's 19 years in office come to an end.
