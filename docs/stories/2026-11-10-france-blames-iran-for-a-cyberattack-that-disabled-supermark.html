France blames Iran for a cyberattack that disabled supermarket fridges

12 reports from 10 outlets · Western media 6 · East Asian media 2 · South Asian media 1 · Middle East media 2 · African media 1

Why This Matters

France accused Iran's Revolutionary Guards of hacking commercial refrigerators and freezers across the country last month. Paris summoned Iran's ambassador, seeks EU sanctions and issued an international arrest warrant. It is the first large-scale online sabotage on French soil and opens a new front with Tehran.

What Others Are Missing

The attack hit food storage, not military targets, which suggests Iran can put pressure on daily life. No public evidence links the hack to the Guard.

What to Watch

EU ambassadors' sanctions talks this week, or a French retaliation, would show whether Paris turns the accusation into joint European action.
