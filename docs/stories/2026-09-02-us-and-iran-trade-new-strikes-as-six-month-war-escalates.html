US and Iran Trade New Strikes as Six-Month War Escalates

September 02, 2026

Why This Matters

US forces struck Iran's Larak Island. Iran fired missiles back at US bases in Jordan and other Gulf targets. Trump had said the war was ending, but fighting just grew again. Oil prices and global bond rates are already reacting.

What Others Are Missing

Most reports on Iranian deaths and strikes come from Iran's state media. No one outside Iran has confirmed these details.

What to Watch

Watch for Iran to strike Gulf shipping or a base in an allied Gulf state within 72 hours.
