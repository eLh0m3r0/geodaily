Russia Strikes Kyiv Again as Peace Push Truce Ends

September 08, 2026

Why This Matters

Russia's three-day pause ended with missile and drone strikes on Kyiv, killing two people and damaging schools and homes. The attack came right after US envoys Witkoff and Kushner left claiming progress. It shows Moscow's actions still don't match the diplomatic talk, raising doubts about any real ceasefire before winter.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on the death toll but underplays Ukrainian officials' open distrust of the envoys' upbeat framing.

What to Watch

Watch whether Russia agrees to another pause or ramps up strikes as US envoys plan next steps.
