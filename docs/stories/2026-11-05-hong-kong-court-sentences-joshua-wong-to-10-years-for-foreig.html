Hong Kong court sentences Joshua Wong to 10 years for foreign collusion

21 reports from 18 outlets · Western media 9 · East Asian media 5 · South Asian media 5 · Middle East media 2

Why This Matters

Wong became a face of Hong Kong's freedom movement in the 2014 Umbrella Movement. He has been jailed since 2020 and was convicted of working with foreign forces under a national safety law. Amnesty International called the sentence a grim reminder of the city's disregard for human rights.

What Others Are Missing

Amnesty said the sentence came months before he would have been due for release. Amnesty called the timing cruel and possibly on purpose.

What to Watch

Whether Britain or the US calls in China's top diplomat or announces sanctions over the sentence will show how far they will go.
