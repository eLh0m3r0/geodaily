France's cost-of-living protests turn into riots and looting

October 18, 2026

Why This Matters

Hundreds of thousands marched over living costs and school conditions, and the unrest became France's worst riots in years. Protesters burned Limoges town hall and looted shops, and police detained about 1,200 people months before the election to replace Macron.

What Others Are Missing

US Vice President JD Vance blamed immigration for the unrest, but French officials have not repeated that claim.

What to Watch

Whether unions call another national march this week will show if the protest wave is still growing.
