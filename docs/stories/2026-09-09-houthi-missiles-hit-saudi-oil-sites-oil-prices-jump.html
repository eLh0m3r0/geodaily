Houthi Missiles Hit Saudi Oil Sites, Oil Prices Jump

September 09, 2026

Why This Matters

Yemen's Houthis fired missiles into Saudi Arabia, hitting an airport, an air base and Aramco oil facilities. It's their heaviest attack on the kingdom in years, wounding 73 people. Brent crude jumped past $100 a barrel for the first time since July on fears of a wider Gulf war.

What Others Are Missing

This follows the deadliest Yemen civil war fighting since 2022. The conflict is widening, not staying contained inside Yemen.

What to Watch

Watch for Saudi retaliatory strikes on Houthi positions in Yemen within days.
