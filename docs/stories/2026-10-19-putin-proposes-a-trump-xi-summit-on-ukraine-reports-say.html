Putin proposes a Trump-Xi summit on Ukraine, reports say

October 19, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump has not agreed to the Kremlin's proposed meeting of Russian, US and Chinese presidents at the APEC summit in Shenzhen on November 18-19. A deal among the three powers could decide Ukraine's future and leave Kyiv and Europe outside the room.

What Others Are Missing

The report relies on unnamed people familiar with the talks, and the White House has not confirmed the plan, so the summit is not yet real.

What to Watch

Whether Trump publicly accepts or rejects the Shenzhen meeting in the next few days will show if he wants China as a broker.
