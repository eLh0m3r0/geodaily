Ethiopian troops seize Mekelle as Tigray fighting flares again

October 04, 2026

Why This Matters

Ethiopian government forces took Mekelle, the capital of Tigray, after a week of battles. The fighting began when Tigrayan fighters attacked neighboring states on September 23. Renewed fighting has raised fears of a repeat of the 2020-2022 civil war, and Eritrea has already cut all ties with Ethiopia.

What Others Are Missing

So far, only the government has described how Mekelle fell. No independent monitors have confirmed where the front line now sits.

What to Watch

If government forces move into western Tigray within 72 hours, that would show this is a full war, not a border clash.
