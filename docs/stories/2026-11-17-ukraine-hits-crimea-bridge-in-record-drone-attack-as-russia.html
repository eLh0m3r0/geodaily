Ukraine hits Crimea bridge in record drone attack as Russia strikes Kyiv

Why This Matters

Russia's defense ministry said Ukraine struck the bridge linking Russia to Crimea for a second day. The ministry said Ukraine also launched 1,300 drones, the war's largest attack. Russian missiles hit two bridges in Kyiv, and the bridge attacks threaten a key Russian supply route. The drone barrage shows Ukraine can reach deep behind the front line.
