Iran says Strait of Hormuz can reopen in two days if US accepts conditions

Why This Matters

Iran's foreign minister said the Strait of Hormuz could reopen within two days. That would happen if Washington recognizes a new shipping route agreed with Oman and closes other routes. The strait is the world's most important oil bottleneck, and reopening it would ease energy markets and lower war risk.
