Yemen's deadliest fighting since 2022 truce kills over 300

September 07, 2026

Why This Matters

Government forces and Houthi rebels have killed over 300 people in days of fighting. It's the worst violence since the 2022 truce ended. Families are fleeing, and the war risks fully reigniting Yemen's civil conflict.

What Others Are Missing

Death tolls come only from military sources on both sides. Independent checks and civilian counts are missing.

What to Watch

Watch for a UN Security Council statement or new Saudi-led ceasefire push within days.
