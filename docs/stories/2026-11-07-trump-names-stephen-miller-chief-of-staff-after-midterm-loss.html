Trump names Stephen Miller chief of staff after midterm losses

November 07, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump replaced chief of staff Susie Wiles with Stephen Miller, who shaped his immigration crackdown, starting Monday. The switch came days after Democrats won the House and Senate. Miller's promotion puts a hardliner in charge of steering Trump's agenda past a Congress that can block it.

What Others Are Missing

Miller, 41, has shaped Trump's immigration policy since the first term. The chief of staff job covers the whole White House staff, not just domestic policy.

What to Watch

By Wednesday, look for new immigration orders or a funding fight with Congress. Either would show Miller is setting policy, not just staff.
