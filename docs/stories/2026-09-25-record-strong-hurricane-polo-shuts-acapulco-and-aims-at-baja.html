Record-strong Hurricane Polo shuts Acapulco and aims at Baja California

September 25, 2026

Why This Matters

Hurricane Polo is a Category 4 storm. It sits off Mexico's Pacific coast. On September 22, its pressure hit 892 millibars. That makes it the second-most intense east Pacific hurricane on record. Acapulco has shut down. Alerts cover Guerrero, Michoacan, Colima and Oaxaca. Very warm Pacific water is fueling the storm.

What Others Are Missing

Reports focus on Acapulco. They miss that Hawaii is tracking its third hurricane threat this year. This Pacific season is unusually busy and tied to record-warm water.

What to Watch

Watch Polo's path toward Baja California. Also watch if Hawaii's third storm, Nolo, shifts toward the islands.
