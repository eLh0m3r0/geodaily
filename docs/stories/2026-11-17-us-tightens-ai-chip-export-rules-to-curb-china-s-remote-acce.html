US tightens AI chip export rules to curb China's remote access

9 reports from 7 outlets · Western media 1 · East Asian media 3 · South Asian media 2 · Middle East media 1 · Regional state media 1 · African media 1

Why This Matters

Washington will require licenses to export advanced AI chips to Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Singapore and Brazil. The rule aims to stop China from renting computer power from far away. It follows the Nvidia scandal over suspected Chinese intelligence ties and comes days before Trump meets Xi Jinping.

What Others Are Missing

A State Department official said Washington would not restrict foreign tech firms in allied countries, so the rule's reach is still unclear.

What to Watch

The first license denials or waivers for Singapore or Malaysia within 72 hours will show how strictly Washington enforces the rule.
