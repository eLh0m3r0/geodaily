Iran and US Trade Biggest Wave of Attacks Yet on Gulf Shipping

September 10, 2026

Why This Matters

Iran says it hit 10 ships near the Strait of Hormuz after the US sank five Iranian tankers. Brent crude broke $100 a barrel for the first time since July. This is the biggest tit-for-tat shipping attack since the war began six months ago, and it puts a critical oil route at real risk.

What Others Are Missing

Much of the ship and casualty count comes from Iran's own military, with little independent confirmation of the damage claimed.

What to Watch

Watch for oil prices to keep rising and shippers to start avoiding the Strait of Hormuz.
