Trump calls for Ukraine to replace Zelensky as US warns Kyiv on strikes in Russia

October 11, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump told reporters Ukraine should get a new president who can make a deal. He blamed Zelensky for high US diesel prices. The US warned Kyiv that strikes inside Russia could cost it shared intelligence. That weakens Ukraine's position one day after Washington lifted sanctions on Russian diesel.

What Others Are Missing

No Ukrainian official has backed the change Trump wants. Ukraine has no elections while martial law remains in force, so there is no legal route to that change.

What to Watch

Whether Kyiv limits its long-range strikes inside Russia within 72 hours, which would show it takes the threat to shared intelligence seriously.
