Saudi-led coalition accuses Houthis of striking Medina power station

October 02, 2026

Why This Matters

The Saudi-led coalition says Houthi drones hit an electricity station feeding the Prophet's Mosque in Medina, Islam's second-holiest city. The Houthis deny it. Muslim governments condemned the attack, which came as fighting between Yemen's government and the rebels intensifies.

What Others Are Missing

The coalition says only one transformer failed and the network kept running. Because the Houthis deny involvement, who fired the drones remains unproven.

What to Watch

Whether Saudi Arabia answers with airstrikes on Sanaa or Hodeidah this week. A wider air campaign would show the Yemen war is escalating again.
