US Missile Strike on Iranian Wedding Kills Civilians, Deepens War

September 03, 2026

Why This Matters

A US missile hit a wedding party in Kuhestak, Iran, killing at least four people, including two children. Iran calls it a war crime. Total deaths from recent US strikes now stand at 18, with 142 wounded, six months into the war. Killing civilians like this could make Iran fight harder and spread the war across the Gulf.

What Others Are Missing

Most reports use Iranian state media for death counts. No one has independently checked the targets or the intent behind the strike.

What to Watch

Watch for Iran to strike back or for the UN Security Council to meet on the war crime claim within 72 hours.
