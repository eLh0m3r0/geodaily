Alberta voters reject separation from Canada, CBC projects

October 20, 2026

Why This Matters

Early results showed about 77% backing 'remain', a clear win for Prime Minister Mark Carney. He manages a trade war with Washington and tries to hold the country together. The campaign exposed real economic anger in Canada's oil heartland, and that argument has not gone away.

What Others Are Missing

CBC called the result with only about a third of ballots counted. So the final margin is still a projection; separatists framed the vote as a protest against federal energy rules.

What to Watch

The final count will matter, and so will whether Alberta's premier and Carney announce a joint energy or budget deal within days.
