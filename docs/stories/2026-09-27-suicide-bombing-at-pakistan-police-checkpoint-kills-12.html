Suicide bombing at Pakistan police checkpoint kills 12

September 27, 2026

Why This Matters

A suicide bomber drove a vehicle full of explosives into a police checkpoint in Dera Ismail Khan. The Pakistani Taliban said it carried out the attack. The blast killed 12 people and wounded 35. The attack shows militants are increasing strikes on security forces.

What Others Are Missing

Most reports focus on the death toll. Gunfire also killed two rescue workers as they moved victims. This shows a second phase of the attack.

What to Watch

Watch for Pakistani military operations in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Also watch for border closures with Afghanistan within days.
