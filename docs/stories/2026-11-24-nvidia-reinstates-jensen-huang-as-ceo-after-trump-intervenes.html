Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after Trump intervenes, lawmakers criticize

Why This Matters

Nvidia brought back CEO Jensen Huang 11 days after he resigned over alleged affairs and claims he broke China export rules. President Trump said he assured the board that Huang was no security risk. Lawmakers from both parties want Congress to tighten China chip rules. The case tests whether a president can override security concerns for a key AI company.
