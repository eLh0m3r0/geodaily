Iran and UAE Hold First Talks Since the War Began

September 13, 2026

Why This Matters

Iran's president met the UAE's crown prince at the BRICS summit in Delhi, their highest-level contact since the war started. Both sides backed a joint call for restraint. It's a signal the Gulf wants to keep the conflict from spreading further.

What Others Are Missing

The meeting produced statements, not commitments. The UAE's August trade suspension with Iran is still in place.

What to Watch

Watch whether the UAE eases its trade suspension with Iran in the coming days.
