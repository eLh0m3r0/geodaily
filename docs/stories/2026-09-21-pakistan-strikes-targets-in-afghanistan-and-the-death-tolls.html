Pakistan strikes targets in Afghanistan, and the death tolls do not match

September 21, 2026

Why This Matters

Pakistan's warplanes hit three sites in eastern Afghanistan on Monday. Islamabad says it killed 28 militants and suicide bombers. The Taliban says three civilians died, including a family in Kunar. These are the first such strikes since June.

What Others Are Missing

Neither side lets independent reporters verify the damage. Pakistan has not described the police-post attack that triggered the strikes, or who it blames.

What to Watch

Watch whether Kabul shows the funerals publicly and whether Pakistan launches a second wave within days.
