Claude maker Anthropic files for a Nasdaq IPO, lifting global markets

October 27, 2026

Why This Matters

Anthropic, the company behind the Claude chatbot, filed to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. Stock markets in Asia and the United States rose on the news. The filing shows fast-growing revenue and large losses, and it tests how much investors will still pay for AI.

What Others Are Missing

The Japan Times reports the shares are expected to become the market's main measure for the AI boom. So the first weeks of trading will matter well beyond one company.

What to Watch

The final offer price and the first day of trading will show whether investors still want to pay high prices for AI companies.
