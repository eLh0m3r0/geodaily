Houthi Offensive Near Red Sea Strait Leaves 120+ Dead

September 05, 2026

Why This Matters

Houthi fighters launched a ground assault to grab control of Bab al-Mandab, the strait guarding the Red Sea's southern entrance. More than 120 people died in two days, the worst fighting there in years. If Houthis win, they could again threaten commercial ships passing through, disrupting global trade routes that took months to reopen.

What Others Are Missing

Most coverage counts bodies but skips what a Houthi win would mean for shipping. Yemeni government sources dominate casualty claims.

What to Watch

Watch for more government reinforcements reaching Taiz, and possible Houthi attacks on ships within 72 hours.
