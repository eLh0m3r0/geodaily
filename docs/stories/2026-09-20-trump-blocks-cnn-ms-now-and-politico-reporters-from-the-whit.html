Trump blocks CNN, MS NOW and Politico reporters from the White House

September 20, 2026

Why This Matters

Journalists from three outlets were denied entry to White House grounds after Trump announced bans on them. Other US newsrooms called the move unconstitutional and an attack on a free press. It tests how far a president can legally restrict coverage of himself.

What Others Are Missing

The White House has published no written rules for the ban. It is unclear who is next or how an outlet could appeal.

What to Watch

Watch whether a banned outlet sues, and whether the White House press corps pushes back as a group.
