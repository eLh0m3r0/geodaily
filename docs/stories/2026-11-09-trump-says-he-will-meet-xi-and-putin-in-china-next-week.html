Trump says he will meet Xi and Putin in China next week

Why This Matters

President Trump said he expects to meet Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin at next week's APEC summit in Shenzhen. He said the talks would discuss denuclearisation, or removing nuclear weapons, and ending the Iran war. That could be a major-power effort to settle a conflict that has disrupted oil shipments.

The key question is whether Beijing or Moscow confirms the three-way meeting before APEC opens. Silence would suggest the plan is still not final.
