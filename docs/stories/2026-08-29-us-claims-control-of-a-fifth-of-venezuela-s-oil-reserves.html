US Claims Control of a Fifth of Venezuela's Oil Reserves

August 29, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump says the US now controls over 65 billion barrels of Venezuelan oil. He calls it the biggest oil deal ever. This could change US fuel prices and give America more power over Venezuela's economy. Trump shared almost no details about the terms or legality.

What Others Are Missing

No one explained who owns what, how Venezuela's interim government agreed, or what happens to regular Venezuelans' oil money.

What to Watch

Watch for contract details, OPEC's response, and whether Venezuela's opposition challenges if the deal is legal.
