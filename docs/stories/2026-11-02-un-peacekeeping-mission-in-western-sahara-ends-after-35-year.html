UN peacekeeping mission in Western Sahara ends after 35 years

November 02, 2026

Why This Matters

After a US-Russia standoff, the Security Council let the 35-year mission expire. Its exit removes the main international monitor from a disputed territory where Morocco and the Algeria-backed Polisario Front remain deadlocked.

What Others Are Missing

Morocco welcomed the end, while the Polisario Front called it unacceptable; no new UN monitor is in place.

What to Watch

If Morocco and the Polisario Front announce talks or new clashes within 72 hours, it will show whether the mission's exit changes the frozen conflict.
