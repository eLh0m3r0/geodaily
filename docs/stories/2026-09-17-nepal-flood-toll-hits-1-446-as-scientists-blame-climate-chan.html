Nepal flood toll hits 1,446 as scientists blame climate change

September 17, 2026

Why This Matters

Scientists say climate change very likely helped trigger the late-August glacier collapse and floods in Nepal. The toll is now 1,446 dead and 6,669 missing. India and China are racing to send aid, turning disaster relief into diplomacy.

What Others Are Missing

Much coverage blames China or missing data. Reporting from Kathmandu points instead at Nepal's own failure to act on warnings.

What to Watch

Watch whether Nepal's government publishes a formal review of its warning system before donor talks.
