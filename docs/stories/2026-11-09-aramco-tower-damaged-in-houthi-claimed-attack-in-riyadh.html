Aramco tower damaged in Houthi-claimed attack in Riyadh

November 09, 2026

Why This Matters

Saudi Arabia said a Houthi attack damaged an Aramco skyscraper in Riyadh's financial district, with no casualties. The strike hit the capital's business core and a symbol of the oil giant. It raised risks to Saudi infrastructure and regional shipping.

What Others Are Missing

Saudi Arabia called the damage minor and said it intercepted missiles. Houthis claimed responsibility, and Yemeni government forces reported a separate offensive that killed or wounded 267 Houthi fighters.

What to Watch

Saudi Arabia may retaliate with new airstrikes on Houthi targets within 72 hours. Or it may limit its response to air defenses.
