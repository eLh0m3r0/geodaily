US sets Russia-Ukraine talks for Abu Dhabi on November 10-11

November 03, 2026

Why This Matters

The US will bring Russian and Ukrainian delegations together in Abu Dhabi on Nov. 10-11. They will discuss ending the war, post-war security and how to lower tensions. US envoy Steve Witkoff announced the working-level talks, the first such three-way meeting since February.

What Others Are Missing

The talks involve officials, not a summit, and no ceasefire has been agreed. Witkoff and Jared Kushner are expected to represent Washington.

What to Watch

Whether Moscow sends a senior delegation to Abu Dhabi will show if the Kremlin is serious about talks.
