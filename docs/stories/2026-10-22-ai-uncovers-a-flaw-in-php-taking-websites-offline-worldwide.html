AI uncovers a flaw in PHP, taking websites offline worldwide

October 22, 2026

Why This Matters

Computer security firms used AI to find two critical flaws in PHP, a decades-old language that helps run about 70% of the world's websites. Governments and companies took sites offline to fix them before hackers could use the bugs.

What Others Are Missing

No one has confirmed a hack yet, and the flaws went unnoticed for years. Attackers may have found them before this week's disclosure.

What to Watch

Whether any hacked site appears in the next three days will show if criminals can use the flaws faster than companies fix them.
