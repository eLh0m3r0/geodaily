Trump Rejects Iran's Truce Offer and Plans to Resume Bombing

September 26, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump rejected Iran's offer to reopen a key oil route and stop fighting in exchange for lifting US sanctions. He told aides he expects to bomb Iran again after November's elections. Restarting the war could send global oil prices higher.

What Others Are Missing

Every report traces to one Wall Street Journal story citing unnamed US officials. Iran has not confirmed the offer's terms, so the truce details stay unverified.

What to Watch

Watch whether tanker traffic through the Strait of Hormuz slows or shipping insurance costs jump in the next few days.
