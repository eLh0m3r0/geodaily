Himalayan Flash Flood Kills Hundreds, Leaves Over 1,400 Missing

August 28, 2026

Why This Matters

A flash flood on the Nepal-Tibet border has killed more than 360 people. Over 1,400 are missing, including 700 foreigners from more than 30 countries. Rescue crews had to pause work when an overflowing lake threatened new floods.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on the death toll but gives little detail on the glacial lake that triggered the flood or the risk of it happening again.

What to Watch

Watch for an updated death toll and word on the fate of missing foreign tourists within 72 hours.
