Suspected Sabotage Cripples Dutch Rail Network at Rush Hour

September 16, 2026

Why This Matters

Someone placed metal pipes at about 30 spots on Dutch train tracks. This stopped train service across the country. ProRail, the rail operator, says it thinks someone did this on purpose. It happened during a string of similar incidents across Europe blamed on people linked to Russia.

What Others Are Missing

No news outlet has named a suspect yet. No one has confirmed if a country was involved.

What to Watch

Dutch police may name a suspect or country within days.
