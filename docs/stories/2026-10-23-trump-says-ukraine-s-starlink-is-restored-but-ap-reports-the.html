Trump says Ukraine's Starlink is restored, but AP reports the speed cap remains

October 23, 2026

Why This Matters

President Trump said Elon Musk had restored Ukraine's Starshield access. A senior European official told the AP the speed limit is still in place. Ukraine's military uses these satellite links to fly drones and strike Russian targets. The conflicting US accounts leave Kyiv unsure who controls a system its army depends on every day.

What Others Are Missing

Musk has said the US military decided the limits, and then the president did. That claim would put a private company's network under direct White House control in wartime.

What to Watch

A Pentagon or SpaceX statement naming who ordered the limits, or Ukrainian reports of new drone failures, would show if Trump's account was accurate.
