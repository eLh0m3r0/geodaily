Supertanker with 2 million barrels hits mine in Hormuz, Iran's Guard says

October 17, 2026

Why This Matters

Iran's Revolutionary Guard says a supertanker carrying more than 2 million barrels of crude hit a sea mine. The Guard says it caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz. The US has two carriers in the Arabian Sea, and Trump has not ruled out wider strikes after the midterms.

What Others Are Missing

No independent source has confirmed the mine claim, which comes from Iran's Revolutionary Guard. Shipping data from Reuters says Iran has exported some fuel since July despite the US blockade.

What to Watch

Whether insurers raise war-risk insurance rates for Hormuz tankers in the next three days, which would show shipping is already pulling back.
