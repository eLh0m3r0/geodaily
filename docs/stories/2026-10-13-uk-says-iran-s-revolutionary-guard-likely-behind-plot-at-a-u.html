UK says Iran's Revolutionary Guard likely behind plot at a US bomber base

October 13, 2026

Why This Matters

Prime Minister Andy Burnham told parliament a suspected plot at RAF Fairford was 'highly likely' coordinated in Iran. He said it was almost certainly directed by the Revolutionary Guard. Police arrested five British men near the base, which had hosted US bombers used to strike Iran.

What Others Are Missing

Iran has not responded publicly, and Britain has not said whether the five men in custody will be charged. The base's US bombers left after Trump cited an Iran-linked threat.

What to Watch

Whether Britain charges the five men, and how Tehran responds, will show if this becomes a formal UK-Iran standoff.
