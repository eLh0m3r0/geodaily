Serbia's Vučić concedes defeat to a student-led movement

October 26, 2026

Why This Matters

Vladimir Putin had endorsed Aleksandar Vučić, yet Serbia's dominant leader for over a decade conceded defeat in Sunday's snap parliament election. A student-led movement spent nearly two years protesting his government and will try to form a government instead.

What Others Are Missing

Vučić left the presidency to run for prime minister, so this defeat ends his formal hold on power. The student coalition has never governed and has no agreed governing plan.

What to Watch

Who the student movement picks as prime minister, and whether Vučić's party tries to block the handover in parliament.
