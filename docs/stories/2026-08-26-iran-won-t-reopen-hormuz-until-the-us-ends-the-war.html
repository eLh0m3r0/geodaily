Iran Won't Reopen Hormuz Until the US Ends the War

August 26, 2026

Why This Matters

Iran and Oman are talking about a temporary safe path and a plan to clear mines from the Strait of Hormuz. Iran says it won't reopen the waterway until the US ends the war. This keeps a fifth of the world's oil supply blocked.

What Others Are Missing

Most news treats this as just a shipping issue. It misses that Iran is using the strait to pressure the US over sanctions.

What to Watch

Watch for Iran and Oman to jointly announce real mine-clearing work within 72 hours.
