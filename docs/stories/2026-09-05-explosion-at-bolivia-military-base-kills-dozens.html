Explosion at Bolivia Military Base Kills Dozens

September 05, 2026

Why This Matters

A blast tore through a fireworks stockpile at Bolivia's Viacha army base, killing at least 10 people. Dozens more are hurt and some remain missing. The disaster raises hard questions about storing explosives near a populated area.

What Others Are Missing

Death tolls vary wildly between outlets, from 2 to 10 dead, showing how confused the first reports were.

What to Watch

Expect Bolivia's defense ministry to release an updated casualty count and cause within days.
