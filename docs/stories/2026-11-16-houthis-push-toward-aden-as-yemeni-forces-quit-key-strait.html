Houthis push toward Aden as Yemeni forces quit key strait

Why This Matters

Forces loyal to Yemen's internationally recognized government pulled back from the Bab el-Mandeb strait. Houthi fighters took two districts in Lahj province and are now about 60km from Aden, the government's temporary capital. Iran fired drones at US bases in Bahrain at the same time. Tehran is now pressing Washington and its Gulf allies on two fronts.
