Brazil votes as Lula and Flávio Bolsonaro run nearly even

October 04, 2026

Why This Matters

Brazilians vote Sunday in a presidential race that polls call a tie. The race is between President Lula da Silva and Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, son of the imprisoned former president. The result will shape Amazon policy and Brazil's ties with both Beijing and Washington.

What Others Are Missing

Ballot boxes traveled by boat to remote Indigenous communities. Amazon conservation is a central campaign issue, so the result depends on voters far outside Brazil's big cities.

What to Watch

First-round results due within hours will show whether the race goes to a runoff and whether either side disputes the count.
