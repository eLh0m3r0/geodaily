Myanmar says it will not fully comply with ICJ genocide ruling

6 reports from 5 outlets · Western media 3 · East Asian media 2 · South Asian media 1

Why This Matters

The International Court of Justice found Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya. It is the first time the UN court has held a state responsible for genocide. Myanmar says it will not fully comply, and the court has little power to enforce the ruling. Legal experts say the precedent could strengthen South Africa's genocide case against Israel.

What Others Are Missing

The ruling linked not having a nationality to genocide but did not require citizenship for all Rohingya. The court left that limit open in its ruling.

What to Watch

The UN Security Council will issue a statement on the ruling within 72 hours; silence would show the court's lack of enforcement power.
