Riyadh airport strike kills 12 as Trump weighs a US role

October 11, 2026

Why This Matters

A missile hit the passenger terminal at Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport on Saturday. It killed 12 people from 11 countries and wounded more than 300, the third attack on the airport this week. The Saudi-led coalition blames Yemen's Houthis and promises a decisive response. President Trump says he is weighing a US role.

What Others Are Missing

Nobody outside the coalition has confirmed who fired the missile. The claim that Houthis did it comes only from Saudi and coalition statements. Riyadh is preparing to host major energy and investment conferences.

What to Watch

A coalition strike inside Yemen within 72 hours, or a US announcement of direct support, would show whether this widens into a US-backed campaign.
