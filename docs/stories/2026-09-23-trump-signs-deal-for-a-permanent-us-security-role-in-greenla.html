Trump signs deal for a permanent US security role in Greenland

September 23, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump says the US won 'permanent control over security' in Greenland. It will build a large military presence there. Greenland is a Danish territory on the shortest route between Russia and the US. The deal ends months of threats to seize the island.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on the signing. Greenlanders say trust in Washington is still shaky, and nobody has explained what 'permanent control' means in practice.

What to Watch

Watch whether Washington names specific bases or troop numbers in Greenland this week, and how Russia and Denmark respond.
