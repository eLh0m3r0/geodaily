Trump threatens to 'annihilate' Iran while UN talks continue

September 23, 2026

Why This Matters

Trump told the UN that Iran can make a deal or face being 'annihilated.' He also said an agreement could come soon. Iran's armed forces dismissed the threat as propaganda. Diplomats are still pushing for a deal at the General Assembly.

What Others Are Missing

The speech mixed a threat experts call a possible war crime with hints of a near deal. Iran has not publicly accepted any terms.

What to Watch

Watch for another US-Iran meeting at the UN this week, and whether Tehran softens its demands.
