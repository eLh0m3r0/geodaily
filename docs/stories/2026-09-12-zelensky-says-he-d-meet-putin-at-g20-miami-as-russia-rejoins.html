Zelensky Says He'd Meet Putin at G20 Miami as Russia Rejoins Talks

September 12, 2026

Why This Matters

Zelensky said he is willing to meet Putin in person at the December G20 summit in Miami. Separately, a Russian official will attend a G20 energy meeting in Houston next week. This is the biggest diplomatic opening in months, even though the war continues.

What Others Are Missing

A meeting offer is not a ceasefire. Past Zelensky-Putin offers have fallen apart quickly with no real follow-through.

What to Watch

Watch for the Kremlin's response to Zelensky's offer and confirmation of Russia's Houston attendance.
