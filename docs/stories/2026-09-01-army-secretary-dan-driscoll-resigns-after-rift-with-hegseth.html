Army Secretary Dan Driscoll Resigns After Rift With Hegseth

September 01, 2026

Why This Matters

The Army just lost its top civilian leader during a live conflict with Iran. Driscoll and Defense Secretary Hegseth had clashed for months over Hegseth's push to cut top generals. The Army now has a leadership gap at a dangerous moment.

What Others Are Missing

Most reports lean on one Wall Street Journal story. The White House has not confirmed the real reason Driscoll left.
