Yemen battle kills up to 169 as Houthis push toward the Red Sea

September 23, 2026

Why This Matters

Houthi forces and the Saudi-backed Yemeni government are fighting for strategic heights near the Red Sea. Up to 169 people have been killed in three days. Thousands of families are fleeing, many for the second or third time.

What Others Are Missing

Death tolls differ by source and are hard to check. Saudi Arabia's king called the Houthis a 'terrorist militia,' and Britain is now refuelling Saudi jets.

What to Watch

Watch whether the Houthis take the Taiz heights and if attacks on Red Sea shipping restart.
