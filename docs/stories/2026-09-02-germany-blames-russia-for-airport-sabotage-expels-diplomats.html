Germany Blames Russia for Airport Sabotage, Expels Diplomats

September 02, 2026

Why This Matters

Germany says Russia tried to blow up a Ukrainian cargo plane at Leipzig airport using a drone packed with explosives. Berlin kicked out diplomats and tightened visa rules. This is Germany's strongest public claim yet about Russian sabotage on its own soil.

What Others Are Missing

Germany hasn't shown lab evidence linking the drone to Russian intelligence. The claim relies only on official statements.

What to Watch

Watch for other EU countries to follow Germany by expelling diplomats or tightening airport security.
