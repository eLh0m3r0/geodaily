Bulgaria elects president who favors dialogue with Moscow

November 02, 2026

Why This Matters

Iliana Yotova, who favors talks with Moscow, won Bulgaria's runoff vote against a banker who backs the West. The result puts a Russia-friendly figure in the presidency of an EU and NATO member as Black Sea drone attacks raise security fears.

What Others Are Missing

The presidency is mostly ceremonial, but Yotova is Bulgaria's first elected female president. She is also an ally of Prime Minister Rumen Radev, and that may boost his power.

What to Watch

If Yotova calls for easing sanctions on Russia within 72 hours, it will show how far she will push Moscow-friendly policies.
