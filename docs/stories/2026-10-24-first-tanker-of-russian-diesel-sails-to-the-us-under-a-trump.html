First tanker of Russian diesel sails to the US under a Trump-Putin deal

October 24, 2026

Why This Matters

A tanker carrying 40,000 tons of Russian diesel left the Baltic port of Primorsk for the US East Coast, the first shipment under an energy deal between Trump and Putin. Washington eased sanctions to allow it, shocking Ukraine and European allies.

What Others Are Missing

Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said shipments will continue on a regular schedule, a sign the arrangement is meant to outlast the first tanker.

What to Watch

Whether a second tanker loads at Primorsk in the next 72 hours will show if this is a one-off or a steady trade.
