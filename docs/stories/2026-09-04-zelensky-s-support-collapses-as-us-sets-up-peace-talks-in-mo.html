Zelensky's Support Collapses as US Sets Up Peace Talks in Moscow and Kyiv

September 04, 2026

Why This Matters

Zelensky's approval has crashed after corruption scandals and a shootout between his own security agencies. Now the US says it will hold separate talks in both Moscow and Kyiv within days. A weakened Zelensky negotiating an endgame to the war could change what Ukraine is forced to accept, with consequences for European security for decades.

What Others Are Missing

Coverage focuses on Zelensky's poll numbers, not on what Kyiv might have to give up to get a deal.

What to Watch

Watch for a confirmed date for Witkoff and Kushner's visit to Kyiv.
