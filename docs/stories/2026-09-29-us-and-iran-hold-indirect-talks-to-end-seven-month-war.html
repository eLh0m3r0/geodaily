US and Iran hold indirect talks to end seven-month war

September 29, 2026

Why This Matters

The US and Iran are talking through go-betweens about ending a seven-month war. Iran wants billions in frozen assets freed, oil sanctions ended, and the US port blockade lifted. A deal could calm oil markets. But the two sides remain far apart.

What Others Are Missing

The talks are indirect. US midterm elections may make Washington unwilling to compromise before November.

What to Watch

Watch whether mediators announce direct talks or another round within 72 hours.
