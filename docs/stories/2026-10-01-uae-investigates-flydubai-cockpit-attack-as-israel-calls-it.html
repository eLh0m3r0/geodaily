UAE investigates Flydubai cockpit attack as Israel calls it a terror plot

October 01, 2026

Why This Matters

The UAE is investigating an attack in a Flydubai cockpit. Israel says the co-pilot tried to crash the Dubai-Tel Aviv flight. Passengers overpowered him, and the plane landed in Saudi Arabia.

What Others Are Missing

Saudi Arabia let the plane with Israeli passengers land but refused an Israeli military aircraft, Haaretz reported, showing the limits of Israel-Saudi cooperation.

What to Watch

UAE findings on the pilot's motive, or whether Saudi Arabia allows Israeli investigators in, will define the next phase.
