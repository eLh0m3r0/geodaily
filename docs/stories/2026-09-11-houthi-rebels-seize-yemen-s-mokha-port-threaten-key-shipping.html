Houthi Rebels Seize Yemen's Mokha Port, Threaten Key Shipping Strait

September 11, 2026

Why This Matters

Houthi forces took over the port city of Mokha. This broke Yemen's shaky 2022 truce. The move brings them closer to controlling the Bab el-Mandeb strait, a route used for 12% of world trade.

What Others Are Missing

Few reports ask if Saudi-backed government forces can fight back, or if war has fully returned.

What to Watch

Watch if Houthi forces move toward the Bab el-Mandeb strait, and how Saudi Arabia reacts militarily.
