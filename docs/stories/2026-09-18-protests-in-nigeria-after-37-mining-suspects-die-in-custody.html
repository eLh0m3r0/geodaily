Protests in Nigeria after 37 mining suspects die in custody

September 18, 2026

Why This Matters

Thirty-seven detainees died in a poorly ventilated cell after a paramilitary raid on illegal gold mines in Niger State. Officials blame disease, but a survivor says they suffocated. Protesters marched overnight.

What Others Are Missing

Illegal mining deaths in Nigeria rarely draw national attention, and no independent autopsy results have been released.

What to Watch

Watch whether Nigeria's government orders an independent investigation into the Niger State deaths this week.
