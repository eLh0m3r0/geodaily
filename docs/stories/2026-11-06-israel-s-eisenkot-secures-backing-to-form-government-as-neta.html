Israel's Eisenkot secures backing to form government as Netanyahu loses allies

November 06, 2026

Why This Matters

Six parties and Israel's largest Arab list have backed Gadi Eisenkot, a former military chief, to form a government. President Isaac Herzog is expected to announce his choice on Sunday. If Herzog chooses him, Eisenkot would replace Benjamin Netanyahu, who has led Israel through three years of war.

What Others Are Missing

Eisenkot's path depends on Arab parties that Netanyahu has accused of taking foreign money. That claim could complicate talks to build a coalition.

What to Watch

Herzog's Sunday announcement will show whether Eisenkot can turn endorsements into a coalition before Netanyahu's allies regroup.
